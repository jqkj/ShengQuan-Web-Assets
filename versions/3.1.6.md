## 中文原版本记录

药剂师获得独立医疗储备。保存军团火种，为后来者安排植入，也面对仪式无法回避的风险。

### 医疗储备：药剂师有了自己的准备

药剂师现在拥有独立的医疗储备，诊疗和手术使用实际执行医师的储备，不再消耗职业进修经验。

储备有上限、初始值和随时间恢复的规则。不同医师之间不共享储备。

### 火种植入：将传承交给下一位战士

符合资格的药剂师可为患者进行分阶段军团火种植入。所用火种决定军团，仪式开始前必须核对患者、执行者、资源和风险。

仪式共十八段；开始时扣除医疗储备和火种。关闭窗口后可稍后返回，但不会刷新整场结果。

### 失败、退款与冷却

火种植入不是无风险的捷径。排斥与死亡具有不同后果；因参与者死亡或离廷而中断时，资源处理也不同。

发起的领主与患者各自进入冷却。请以游戏实际界面与现行说明为准。

### 圣泉功业：暂停不是存档冻结

暂停会立即清空已接和未接的委托，停止发单、任务推进和购买。重新启用后会重新张榜，而不是恢复那些被清空的委托。

凝露、成长与未使用的商品库存仍可保留。

## English historical summary

**Embers Endure** — Apothecaries gain personal medical reserves. Preserve Legion gene-seed, prepare an implantation, and confront the ritual’s irreversible risks.

### Medical reserves: preparation matters

Apothecaries now maintain their own medical reserves. Treatments and surgery use the acting physician’s reserve instead of profession training experience.

Reserves have an individual cap, starting balance and recovery schedule. Physicians do not share the resource.

### Gene-seed implantation: pass on a legacy

An eligible Apothecary may perform a multi-stage implantation on an eligible patient. The chosen gene-seed determines the Legion. Confirm the patient, doctor, resources and risk before beginning.

The ritual has eighteen stages. Reserve and gene-seed costs are committed at the start. Closing the window does not reroll the outcome.

### Failure, refunds and cooldowns

Implantation is not a risk-free upgrade. Rejection and death have different consequences; interruption caused by death or departure follows separate resource rules.

The commissioning ruler and the patient enter cooldowns. Refer to the latest in-game rules for exact constraints.

### Deeds: pausing is not freezing

Pausing clears accepted and unaccepted commissions and stops new offers, progress and purchases. Resuming creates new commissions rather than restoring cleared ones.

Dew, growth and unused stock remain.
