# V3.1.6.3｜CK3 1.21 适配与宝物熔炉修复

2026-11-19 · [工坊 / Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3381939779&l=schinese) · [仓库原稿 / Source notes](https://github.com/jqkj/CK3-ShengQuan-Legend/blob/642f8cca3ed80d666f22d49acac06c5bca627bea/docs/_ledger/release_v1/20261119_v3163_announcement.md)

## 简体中文

圣泉本体迁移到 CK3 1.21 的繁荣、税收、民众好感和控制力规则，并修复精钢装备重铸、预设肖像、京观和誓言板。

### CK3 1.21：从发展度转向繁荣

圣泉祈福、宝物词缀和誓言板委托更新了相关税收、民众好感和繁荣效果；原先影响发展度的相关效果改用繁荣或繁荣增长。京观及其他相关控制力效果也改按新版控制力基准与每月变化处理。

### 宝物熔炉：精钢重铸与耐久

修复部分精钢装备重铸时无法识别类型的问题。对保留耐久的重铸，定型后恢复原有耐久及耐久上限。词缀锁定按钮、槽位分隔线和未选定词缀时的界面也已修复。

### 预设肖像、京观与誓言板

补齐预设肖像数据，没有龙角数据时默认无角，由原有显示规则判定是否佩戴。修正战斗或围城后京观读取交战方和地点的问题，以及新档关闭欧格林规则时的特质清理。誓言板不再提前读取尚未建立的年度配额或初泉进度。

> 这是圣泉本体的 1.21 适配更新，不包含新版骑士团界面，也不代表独立混沌扩展已适配。建议先单独验证圣泉本体，再检查旧档或组合加载。

## English

The core mod moves to CK3 1.21’s prosperity, tax, popular opinion and control rules, with fixes for steel reforging, preset portraits, victory mounds and the Oath Board.

### CK3 1.21: from Development to Prosperity

Sacred Spring blessings, artifact affixes and Oath Board commissions now use updated tax, popular opinion and prosperity effects where applicable. Former development effects now affect prosperity or its growth. Victory mounds and related county effects use the new control baseline and monthly change rules.

### The Forge: steel reforging and durability

The Forge now recognises the item type for affected refined-steel equipment. Reforges that preserve durability restore both the previous durability and its cap after finalisation. Affix lock alignment, slot dividers and empty-selection tooltips were also fixed.

### Preset portraits, victory mounds and the Oath Board

Preset portrait data have been filled in. Portraits without horn data default to no horns and follow the existing visibility rules. Victory mound events now resolve affected participants and locations correctly after battle or siege, and new games clean up Ogryn traits when the rule is disabled. The Oath Board no longer reads an unset annual quota or Spring progress.

> This CK3 1.21 migration is for the core mod. It does not include the new knight-order UI or establish compatibility with the independent Chaos expansion. Test the core mod first before trying an existing save or a combined playset.
