## 中文更新

宝物定型保留原类型，五连与十连不再招募已故候选，圣泉学院把奖励所需的属性和门槛标得更清楚。

### 宝物熔炉：定型保留原类型

普通随身和宫廷宝物抽到「宝物变换」时，定型后仍可使用原栏位。成功定型花费 100 金，耐久按抽中结果结算。若无法识别类型，熔炉会提示，并保留宝物和金币。

升华结果可在当前面板查看；选中另一件未升华宝物后，上一件离开熔炉列表，但仍在宝物库存。抽到损毁则移除原宝物，补偿一块工匠石，通知中会显示石头图标。

### 五连与十连：只招募合格候选

已故候选的卡片仍可查看，但不能单独招募。「全部招募」只接收仍存活且未招募的候选，没有合格候选时按钮置灰。重抽和关闭窗口的清理也会跳过已故候选。

### 圣泉学院：奖励门槛更清楚

特质进度条现在显示对应属性图标。四星管理教育的「管理者」进度按 24 点管理的奖励门槛显示。

年度授奖按钮换用原版线条箭头。刷新学院会清除失效的选中对象，在读、毕业和辍学的有效档案仍可查看。

> 这是主模组的修补公告，不是 CK3 1.21 兼容声明。游戏版本和依赖以当期工坊说明为准。

## English release notes

Reforged artifacts keep their original type, five- and ten-draw recruitment skips deceased candidates, and Academy progress displays its stat requirements more clearly.

### Forge: reforged artifacts keep their type

When a standard inventory or court artifact rolls Artifact Transformation, a successful reforge keeps its original type and slot. It costs 100 gold; durability follows the outcome. If the type cannot be identified, the Forge displays a notice and keeps both the artifact and the gold.

The ascension result remains visible in the current panel. Selecting another unascended artifact removes the previous one from the Forge list, not from your inventory. A destruction result removes the artifact and awards one Artisan Stone, shown in the notification.

### Five and ten draws: recruit only eligible candidates

Deceased candidates remain viewable but cannot be recruited individually. Recruit All only accepts living candidates who have not already been recruited and is disabled when none qualify. Cleanup on reroll or close also skips deceased candidates.

### Academy: clearer reward requirements

Trait progress bars now show the relevant stat icon. For four-star Stewardship education, Administrator progress is displayed against the 24 Stewardship reward threshold.

The annual rewards button now uses the base-game line arrow. Refreshing the Academy clears invalid selections while keeping valid student, graduate, and dropout records available.
