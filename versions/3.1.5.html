中文公告

圣泉功业、精钢军械与宝物能力展示。

揭榜远行，完成托付

持有悬圃核心的领主可以通过功业入口接受委托。年度轮换的任务涵盖护泉、护送、礼器寻回、纠纷调解等题材。

需要亲自远行、作出选择并完成结案或交接。

初泉成长与泉畔援助

功业所得的凝露可用于休整、行装和进修。不同路线与完成方式连接着初泉成长与后续仪式。

精钢军械与宝物画卷

精钢军械扩充为十五件装备，并有组合条件与效果。宝物详情加强了专属能力、套装状态和诅咒代价的展示。

主模组与外观扩展分工

阿斯塔特职业、军团与升格玩法属于主模组；铠甲模型、材质、头盔和理发店外观由独立外观扩展维护；实际必需项目请以当前工坊依赖列表为准。

历史版本回顾，不代表现在所有具体条件仍与当时一致。请优先阅读最新版本的说明。

English release notes

Sacred Spring Deeds, refined-steel armaments and artifact presentation.

Take a commission. Keep the promise.

Rulers with the Xuanpu core can take up rotating Deeds: protecting springs, escorting envoys, recovering relics and resolving disputes.

Travel, choose an outcome and complete the handover or settlement.

Early growth and support

Spend earned Dew on recovery, travel supplies and training. Commission routes connect to early bloodline growth.

Refined steel and artifact details

Fifteen refined-steel armaments with combination requirements were introduced. Artifact details now present unique abilities, sets and cursed trade-offs more clearly.

Core mod versus armour add-on

Professions, Legions and ascension remain in the core mod. Armour models, materials, helmets and barbershop options belong to a separate appearance expansion; check the current Workshop required-items list.

This is an earlier release recap, not a guarantee that all conditions remain unchanged. Consult the latest release first.
