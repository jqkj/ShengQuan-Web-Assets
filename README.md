## 中文介绍

**《圣泉传说》（Sheng Quan Chuan Shuo）** 是一款为《十字军之王 III》（Crusader Kings III）制作的非商业奇幻题材模组。故事围绕**湖水女神、圣泉的庇护与凡人之间的誓约**展开，让王朝经营、角色培养、信仰与战争融入一个不断延伸的奇幻世界。

在这里，圣泉不只是传说中的神迹。你可以召集追随者、培育继承人、建立学院与骑士团，探索圣泉悬圃、宝物与锻造体系，并在阿斯塔特、龙泉等扩展内容中体验不同的角色与军事玩法。

### 主要内容

- **湖水女神与圣泉世界**：圣泉悬圃、信仰叙事，以及与女神意志相联系的角色和事件。
- **角色与王朝**：圣泉征召、血脉传承、学院教育、宫廷职位和人物成长。
- **骑士与战争**：圣泉骑士团的册封、管理、召集和继承，以及特色战斗单位。
- **宝物与日常**：宝物库、熔炉、军械、塔罗牌及其他可探索的互动系统。
- **奇幻扩展**：阿斯塔特职业、龙泉等跨题材内容，为不同路线提供新的体验。

## English Introduction

**Sheng Quan Chuan Shuo** is a non-commercial fantasy mod for **Crusader Kings III**. Centered on the **Lady of the Lake, the sacred springs, and the vows binding mortals to divine power**, it brings a growing fantasy setting into the game's dynastic politics, character development, faith, and warfare.

In this world, the sacred spring is more than a distant legend. Recruit followers, guide the next generation, establish an academy and an order of knights, discover treasures and forge equipment, and explore additional paths through Astartes professions, Longquan, and other fantasy-themed systems.

### Key Features

- **The Lady of the Lake and the Sacred Springs** — a fantasy setting built around the sacred sanctuary, faith, characters, and events.
- **Characters and Dynasties** — recruitment, bloodline legacies, academy education, court roles, and character progression.
- **Knights and Warfare** — the Sacred Spring Knightly Order, including investiture, management, mustering, and succession, alongside distinctive military content.
- **Treasures and Everyday Stories** — a treasury, forge, equipment, tarot readings, and other interactive features.
- **Fantasy Expansions** — Astartes professions, Longquan, and additional paths for roleplaying and warfare.

> Development is ongoing. For current availability, requirements, and gameplay details, please refer to the latest mod version and the official project guide.
