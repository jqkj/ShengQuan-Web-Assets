一｜有人来求助，有人来寻求超越

失去手臂的骑士走进工坊，希望再次握住兵器；一位身体健全的修士也来到这里，希望通过机械改造拥有更强的战力。两个人所追求的并不相同，却可能面对同一位技术军士（00_astartes_techmarine）。机械义肢、义腿和「义眼植入」各有对应身体条件、费用与风险，成功可以帮助人物恢复或改善相应能力，失败却可能带来新的伤害。

技术军士并非按下按钮就把任何人改造成完美机器。手术要检查关系、目标处境与执行者的造诣和个人技术点；尤其是迈入25 XP后掌握的「血肉净化」，能通过特制源液彻底治愈白化病、口吃、巨人症、侏儒、驼背、哮喘与血友病等全部先天遗传缺陷。

二｜每一副新身体都需要继续维护

初次安装义体以后，「多功能组件平台」允许按条件提升已经拥有的机械部位。义眼共分四阶，可逐级检修强化。「模块装载」为具体人物提供额外方向的专业支持，技术军士自身可装满全部模块，他人则限装一件。

技术军士「创造机仆」，为君主或宫廷提供持续5年的高效能服务；机仆能源耗尽后停机，需花费200金币重新补能充能，方可重新部署。

三｜强化既有修士，与初次升格没有混合入口

在资深阶段，技术军士可以为修士加装「神经接口」，这是迈向更高阶机械飞升的必要前置。达到 100 XP 后，技术军士更能主持「原铸化改造」（基础成功率61.6%，依学识提升）。

必须严正分清：原铸化是强化已经属于五大阿斯塔特职业的修士，并不承担普通廷臣的初次阿斯塔特升格。手术有其健康风险与死亡概率，绝非毫无代价的属性增长。

四｜机器能延续功能，人仍须承担选择

达到巅峰造诣的技术军士自身可选择接受「欧姆弥赛亚化身」，彻底完成机械飞升——肉体停止衰老，容貌定格，免疫普通阿斯塔特漫长服役衰老死亡，每隔15年机体自我进化一次，技术点月回复提升至15点。

技术军士的成长由技术学徒（0 XP）、技术军士（25 XP）、连队锻造修士（50 XP）、高级锻造宗师（75 XP）至铸炉之主（100 XP）。钢铁之手军团专长令技术点恢复翻倍。在金属与机械齿轮的咬合中，科技与忠诚铸就了不灭的永恒。

Story, choices and consequences

One courtier comes to the Techmarine's forge hoping to walk again; another has a healthy body but wants to push its abilities further. Both may seek surgery, yet their aims differ. Arm, leg and eye implants treat different parts of the body, and eligibility is not determined solely by having lost a limb. The target's peace, freedom, travel, proximity and relationship conditions matter, while a failed operation can leave new injuries.

A successful implant can be developed further with the Multi-Function Component Platform. Flesh Purification, meanwhile, targets a specified list of bodily traits in one operation; it cannot selectively remove only the entries you dislike. That list even includes traits a player might intentionally wish to keep. Read it before spending the cost, rather than taking the word 'purification' as proof that every result is beneficial.

The forge also serves people beyond the patient. Module Loading offers a random specialisation with different replacement rules for Techmarines and others. Create Servitor makes a new servant character for a chosen purpose—building, paperwork, training, medical support, battle or chanting—and each has its own recipients and ongoing maintenance. Component upgrades spend technical points and gold; servitor recharging only spends gold, and restoration of service waits for the relevant check.

Deeper transformation is divided among Neural Interface, Primaris Conversion and Mechanical Ascension. These are not three buttons that any candidate can press in sequence. Primaris conversion strengthens someone already Astartes and uses the operating Techmarine's expertise; Mechanical Ascension is a path for an eligible Techmarine with an interface, and spends the recipient's technical reserve. The success test and resource holder are different in the two cases.

Successful Mechanical Ascension changes far more than a combat statistic: apparent ageing, fertility, personality and long-term development are affected, and growth continues over years. The character remains vulnerable to violence and other fatal events. Technical points recover separately from experience, with Iron Hands and ascended characters gaining their documented synergies. The Techmarine's value is in planning what to build, whom to transform and how to sustain the results afterward.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

职业能力与操作边界Role abilities & limitations

大多数专业服务消耗执行技术军士的技术点；神经接口与机械飞升消耗接受改造者本人的技术点。

原铸化看执行者学识，机械飞升看接受者学识。两项改造的资源归属和成功依据不能互换。

多功能组件平台的义体升级要金币和技术点，机仆充能只收金币。

专业履历逐步改善建造时间与成本、堡垒、攻城、管理，以及建筑带来的虔诚、收入和威望。

当前技术点储量另有管理与学识收益，消耗后相应储量加成会改变。技术点随时间回复，钢铁之手出身与成功机械飞升各有回复协同。

Most services spend the performing Techmarine’s technical points; Neural Interface and Mechanical Ascension spend the recipient Techmarine’s own reserve.

Primaris conversion tests the operator’s Learning; mechanical ascension tests the recipient’s Learning. Their success checks and resource owners are different.

The Component Platform requires gold and technical points for prosthetic upgrades; recharging a servitor only costs gold.

Professional service improves construction time and costs, fortifications, sieges, Stewardship and some building-related piety, income and prestige.

Current technical reserve offers Stewardship and Learning support. Spending it changes those bonuses; recovery occurs over time, with additional synergies for Iron Hands and successful mechanical ascension.

义肢植入Prosthetic Arm

成功后清除残废，获得机械义肢，并改善健康与勇武；组件质量影响进一步的勇武收益。

  • 消耗金币与执行者的技术点；目标通常须和平、自由、未旅行，并满足关系与距离条件。
  • 可能失败，并留下治疗失败效果、创伤或肢体损害。

On success, remove the Maimed trait, give a mechanical arm and improve health and Prowess. Higher component quality can further enhance Prowess.

  • Costs gold and the operating Techmarine’s technical points; the target generally must be at peace, free, not travelling, and eligible by relationship and distance.
  • Can fail and cause a failed-treatment modifier, trauma or further limb damage.

义腿植入Prosthetic Leg

成功后清除独腿，获得机械义腿，并改善健康与旅行速度；更高性能还帮助军队移动。

  • 消耗金币与执行者的技术点，并检查手术状态、关系和距离。
  • 可能失败，造成创伤或腿部损害。

On success, remove One-Legged, grant a mechanical leg, and improve health and travel speed; higher performance can support army movement.

  • Costs gold and the operator’s technical points and checks surgery, relationship and range conditions.
  • Failure can cause trauma or leg damage.

义眼植入Prosthetic Eye

成功后清除单眼或失明，获得机械义眼，并改善健康、恐怖积累与密谋侦查。

  • 消耗金币与执行者的技术点，并检查手术状态、关系和距离。
  • 义眼会降低大众好感；失败还可能造成创伤或视觉损害。

On success, remove One-Eyed or Blind, grant a mechanical eye and improve health, dread gain and scheme discovery.

  • Costs gold and the operator’s technical points and checks surgery, relationship and range conditions.
  • The eye can lower general opinion; failure may cause trauma or vision damage.

血肉净化Flesh Purification

尝试同时清除目标身上属于净化名单的特质。决定前应把整个名单看完，其中包括可能被你有意保留的“巨人”。

  • 消耗金币与技术点，有成功和失败之分；失败可能留下治疗失败状态和伤势。
  • 不能逐项选择保留其中某些特质；近亲繁殖、低智力、虚弱体质等不在这次手术范围内。

Attempt to remove all traits on the specific purification list currently held by the recipient. The list includes Giant, which some players may wish to preserve.

  • Costs gold and technical points; may fail and cause failed treatment or injury.
  • You cannot choose individual traits to keep from the eligible list. Inbred, low intellect and frailty are not part of this operation.

多功能组件平台Multi-Function Component Platform

为已有义肢、义腿或义眼升级，直至各组件性能上限；也可为能源不足的机仆补充能源。执行维护的技术军士获得相应历练或虔诚回报。

  • 义体升级需要金币与执行者的技术点，并要求目标已经装有相应组件。
  • 为机仆充能只收金币，不扣技术点；它的服务要等后续运行检查才恢复。

Upgrade an installed arm, leg or eye component to its cap, or recharge a servitor lacking energy. Technical services give the Techmarine their relevant experience or piety reward.

  • Prosthetic upgrading requires gold, the operator’s technical points and an installed matching component.
  • Servitor recharge only costs gold, not technical points; restored service resumes after a later update check.

模块装载Module Loading

随机获得一种尚可装载的专业模块。技术军士可逐步兼收全部六种；其他角色的五类常规模块彼此替换。

  • 消耗金币与技术点，不能点选本次结果。
  • 罕见的圣者模块另行保留，因此非技术军士也可能同时拥有圣者模块和一种常规模块。

Randomly add an eligible professional module. Techmarines may eventually combine all six; ordinary recipients replace one of five regular modules with another.

  • Costs gold and technical points and cannot select the random result.
  • A rare Saint module is separately preserved, so a non-Techmarine can hold it alongside one ordinary module.

创造机仆Create Servitor

资深技术军士按你选择的用途创造新的机仆人物，成品质量影响服务强度。你可以接纳它，也可以拒绝并销毁；诵经机仆需要更高造诣。

  • 消耗金币与技术点，生成的是新人物，不会自动把选中的囚犯或廷臣改造成机仆。
  • 能源随时间耗尽，停机后失去服务效益；充能后等待运行检查恢复。部署收益本身有期限。
  • 机仆可以离廷，死亡也会影响服务；后来入廷的人不会自动永久获得全部既有部署收益。
  • 机仆不能生育，也不能担任军队指挥官。战斗机仆提供统治者的军事修正，不直接生成一支新军队。

An experienced Techmarine creates a new servitor character for an assigned service. Quality affects its bonus. Accept the result or refuse and destroy it; the chanting type needs higher mastery.

  • Costs gold and technical points. It creates a new person rather than converting the selected prisoner or courtier.
  • Energy declines and the service stops when depleted; recharging waits for the next running check. Deployment bonuses have a time limit.
  • A servitor can leave court or die. New arrivals do not automatically inherit all historical deployments.
  • Servitors cannot reproduce or command armies. A Combat Servitor grants the ruler a military modifier; it does not directly summon a regiment.

神经接口Neural Interface

为具备经验的技术军士安装接口，提高六项角色能力，并给予相应历练。接口也是机械飞升的前置条件。

  • 费用由玩家承担，技术点取自接受接口的技术军士本人。
  • 接受者仍须满足手术状态与关系要求。

Install an interface in an experienced Techmarine, improving all six character attributes and giving corresponding experience. This is also required for Mechanical Ascension.

  • The ruler pays money, while technical points come from the Techmarine receiving the interface.
  • Recipient must meet surgery-state and relationship requirements.

原铸化阿斯塔特Primaris Astartes Conversion

成功后同时获得原铸强化与神经接口，改善各项能力和健康。

  • 对象须已经属于五种阿斯塔特职业之一，尚未原铸且未机械飞升。它不承担普通人的初次升格，也不重新选择职业。
  • 消耗金币与执行技术军士的技术点，由执行者的学识影响成败。
  • 失败可能损害智力、体质并造成创伤，严重时死亡；失败不会自动免除资源损失。

Strengthen an already existing Astartes; on success grant Primaris enhancements and a neural interface, improving abilities and health.

  • Target must already belong to one of five Astartes professions, not yet be Primaris or mechanically ascended. This is not the initial ascension of an ordinary courtier and does not choose a new profession.
  • Uses gold and the operating Techmarine’s points; the operator’s Learning affects success.
  • Failure can harm intellect, physique or health, cause injuries or death; costs are not automatically waived.

欧姆弥赛亚化身Avatar of the Omnissiah

成功者获得真正的永生特质，外观年龄停止增长，不再生育，部分既有性格被清除。此后随年份积累机械成长，在对应阶段增加六项能力，技术点回复也得到加强。

  • 接受者须为已装有神经接口的技术军士，并满足相应工艺条件。
  • 消耗金币与接受飞升者本人的技术点，成败也看这名接受者的学识；失败会留下伤害。
  • 成功者脱离普通阿斯塔特的漫长服役死亡检查，仍可能死于暴力或其他致死事件。

An eligible Techmarine can attempt Mechanical Ascension. Success grants genuine immortality from ordinary ageing, stops visual ageing and reproduction, removes specified personality traits and begins yearly mechanical growth in all six stats, with improved technical recovery.

  • Recipient must be a Techmarine already fitted with the neural interface and satisfy the craft requirements.
  • Uses gold and the recipient’s own technical points; success uses that recipient’s Learning. Failure can injure.
  • Successful characters leave ordinary long-service ageing death checks, but can still die by violence and other fatal causes.
