一｜有些伤口并不出现在身体上

一位廷臣总因怒火与同伴争吵，另一位靠不良习惯缓解压力，还有人带着深重的罪名来到宫廷。阿斯塔特牧师（00_astartes_acolyte）面对的不是同一种肉体疾病，而是人物性格、应对习惯和社会罪责之间不同的困境。「个性熔铸」与「高阶个性熔铸」处理特定性格，「羔羊归途」面对不良应对习惯，「罪与罚」及「轻罪忏悔」则各自针对特定罪名特质。

选择正确的入口，比把每个问题都交给同一种“净化”更重要。牧师不能为任意人物选择一整套完美性格，普通与高阶熔铸的结果范围不同，而且每次仍会处理符合条件的具体特质。一次改变可能带来新的优点，也可能留下你没有预料到的结果。

二｜忏悔有其力量，也有不可越过的界线

罪名相关服务能够处理规则所列的特质，却不会让所有过去的犯罪记录、秘密或知情人的记忆随之消散。对于符合条件的非玩家目标，某些牧师服务还可能建立牵制关系。角色在仪式之后也许获得了重新开始的机会，但这个机会是否被滥用、是否值得信任，仍是未来宫廷政治的一部分。

牧师的专业资历影响能承担哪些服务，领主还须支付对应金币；不能因为同在圣堂，就把不同严重程度的过错当成同一种忏悔草率处理。

三｜这份职责也能延伸到土地上

当随军牧师达到最高造诣，圣洁之主能够为领主直属的公国举行神圣的「赐福仪式」。同一种效果覆盖该公国法理范围内的全部伯爵领，赋予持续数年的税收繁荣、堡垒坚固或发展度跃升。

必须选择领主亲自持有的合格公国头衔，仪式具有冷却间隔。其作用范围由公国法理决定，既不等于整个领国，也不限于直辖的单一城堡。

四｜给予改变的机会，无法保证改变的结局

牧师的阶位由侍僧（0 XP）、牧师（25 XP）、连队牧师（50 XP）、隐修长（75 XP）至圣洁之主（100 XP）。他的圣锤与圣典为军团守住了信仰的火种，但人的选择终究属于当事人自己。

赋予一个人忏悔与新生的机会，是为了让他在未来的战阵与治理中赎清前愆。维系信仰的纯洁与公义，正是随军牧师不可动摇的永恒天职。

Story, choices and consequences

An Astartes may keep the oath in battle and still bring anger, destructive habits or the burden of a criminal trait home to court. A Chaplain addresses those different problems through different services. Personality Reforging changes one qualifying trait into a random compatible result; advanced reforging narrows that result toward a particular virtue range. Neither allows you to design an exact personality, and the ordinary process can still produce an unfavourable trait.

Return of the Lamb deals with a listed coping habit, not with an entire character's mind or faith. The criminal-trait services—Crime and Punishment and Minor Penance—each remove one qualifying trait from their own lists. This is not an erasure of secrets, other criminal records or the memories of everybody who knows what happened. Serving a non-player recipient can also produce a hook, so the spiritual choice has political consequences.

When a Chaplain's mastery is sufficient, the work may extend from one person to a duchy. The Duchy Blessing ritual targets a qualifying duchy title you actually hold and randomly brings tax, fortification or development benefits to counties in its de jure scope. It is not a realm-wide aura, nor is it restricted to the counties you personally hold within the duchy.

Personal services check the real Chaplain's mastery and their recipient's relationship, and most of those services charge gold. The duchy ritual has its own rules and no separate interaction gold cost. Performing services can grow professional experience; later service and piety bring personal advantages over time. A Chaplain's calling is to help an oath endure, not to claim that one rite can rewrite every part of a life.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

职业能力与操作边界Role abilities & limitations

性格、习惯与忏悔服务收取金币，同时检查实际牧师的造诣。赐福仪式不另收这一互动的金币费用。

自己、宫廷人物、囚犯、封臣及符合家族首领条件的宗亲是否能接受服务，须依具体确认窗判断，不能把一种服务的关系范围套到所有入口。

个人服务使实际完成的牧师获得历练；已经登峰造极者获得对应虔诚回报。

履历逐步改善同信仰关系、同信仰征召兵补充与虔诚获取，并增强压力管理。虔诚还会在对应阶段成为勇武、学识、军事或外交的来源。

这些阶段优势属于修士本人，不能将每一项都理解为自动传递给整座宫廷的光环。

Personality, habit and penance services charge gold and require suitable mastery. The duchy blessing ritual has no separate gold charge for that interaction.

Targets among self, courtiers, prisoners, vassals and qualifying relatives depend on the specific interaction. Do not copy one service’s relationship limits to another.

Completed personal services give the performing Chaplain professional experience; fully accomplished members receive the corresponding piety reward.

Later service improves same-faith relations, same-faith levy reinforcement, piety and stress management. Piety can contribute to Prowess, Learning, Martial or Diplomacy at the appropriate stages.

These are the Chaplain’s personal career effects; they do not all automatically become court-wide bonuses.

个性熔铸Personality Reforging

处理目标身上的一种合格性格，再随机塑造一种能与其剩余性格相容的结果。

  • 一次只处理一项，不能指定结果；普通熔铸可能产生不利性格。
  • 已有性格会排除相斥结果，目标关系和牧师造诣须符合本项服务。

Remove one qualifying personality trait and randomly grant a compatible result that does not conflict with remaining traits.

  • One eligible trait at a time; the result cannot be selected and can be unfavourable.
  • Recipient relationship and Chaplain experience must qualify.

高阶个性熔铸Advanced Personality Reforging

同样先处理一项合格性格，再从相容的指定美德范围中随机取得一种。

  • 需要更有造诣的牧师，仍一次只处理一项，不能点选具体美德。
  • 高级入口覆盖多变、安于现状、固执与轻信，但不包含普通入口中的暴食。

Remove one qualifying trait and randomly replace it with a compatible item from the specified virtue pool.

  • Requires a more accomplished Chaplain and still cannot select a particular virtue.
  • The advanced operation covers Fickle, Content, Stubborn and Trusting, but not the Gluttonous entry covered in ordinary reforging.

羔羊归途Return of the Lamb

每次处理一项符合范围的应对习惯。为非玩家人物完成这项服务时，你还会获得一份弱牵制。

  • 这项服务处理明确列出的习惯，不改变目标信仰，也不覆盖全部精神疾病。
  • 需要合格牧师、金币及允许关系内的目标。

Remove one listed coping trait from an eligible recipient. Serving someone other than the player grants a weak hook.

  • Does not change the recipient’s faith or cure all mental conditions.
  • Requires an eligible Chaplain, gold and permitted recipient relationship.

罪与罚Crime and Punishment

从弑亲、食人、绝罚与谋杀等符合条件的罪名特质中处理一项。为非玩家人物服务时，你获得强牵制。

  • 收取金币，并要求牧师达到相应造诣。
  • 移除的是相关特质，不会整体抹去既有秘密、所有人的记忆或其他独立犯罪记录。

Remove one eligible criminal trait from a list including Kinslayer, Cannibal, Excommunicated and Murderer; service to a non-player recipient gives a strong hook.

  • Costs gold and requires appropriate Chaplain mastery.
  • Removing the trait does not erase all existing secrets, memories or independent criminal records.

轻罪忏悔Minor Penance

每次从游戏命名的通奸者、私通者、“变态”、乱伦者与“鸡奸者”特质中处理一项。为非玩家人物服务时，你获得弱牵制。

  • 收取金币，并要求牧师达到相应造诣。
  • 同样只处理匹配特质，秘密和其他独立记录不会因此全部消失。

Remove one eligible trait from the game-defined Adulterer, Fornicator, Deviant, Incestuous and Sodomite set; a non-player recipient gives a weak hook.

  • Costs gold and requires corresponding Chaplain mastery.
  • Removes the matching trait only; other secrets or records remain.

赐福仪式Duchy Blessing

最资深的牧师随机施予税收、堡垒或发展度中的一种祝福，同一种效果覆盖该公国法理范围内的全部伯爵领，并持续一段时间。

  • 必须选择你持有的合格公国头衔，有再次举行间隔。
  • 本项互动不另收金币；作用范围由公国法理决定，既不等于整个领国，也不限于你直辖的地产。

A senior Chaplain randomly grants tax, fortification or development benefits to all counties within the selected duchy title’s de jure scope for a limited time.

  • Select an eligible duchy title you actually hold; the ritual has a reuse interval.
  • No separate gold charge for this interaction. Scope is de jure duchy counties, not the entire realm or just personally held counties.
