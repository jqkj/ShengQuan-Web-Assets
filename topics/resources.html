货币、威望与圣泉标识CK3 Resource & Icon Reference

参照 CK3 本体与圣泉图标注册，整理游戏货币、角色属性和模组自定义资源的区别。Verified registries for currencies, attributes and mod-specific resources.

资源图鉴Resource guide

资源名称、贴图文件路径源自 CK3 本体 GUI 和圣泉模组的注册文件。其中四种资源展示与注册路径核对过的游戏 PNG 预览，其余使用本站自绘示意图；PNG 并非 DDS 二进制原文件；不以本站图例代替游戏里的费用、资格或奖励结算。Names and source paths come from CK3 and Sacred Spring GUI registries. Four resources show PNG references matched to registered image filenames; other symbols remain site-drawn. These are not DDS binary files. Exact costs and outcomes remain in-game decisions.

金币Gold

Crusader Kings III · @gold_icon!

王朝经营与部分互动使用的金币。A widely used currency in realm management and interactions.

gfx/interface/icons/icon_gold.dds

威望Prestige

Crusader Kings III · @prestige_icon!

角色的世俗声望；例如骑士团召集可能花费威望。An individual's secular standing, potentially used to rally knights.

gfx/interface/icons/modifiers/icon_prestige_01.dds

虔诚Piety

Crusader Kings III · @piety_icon!

宗教资源；游戏根据宗教有不同的图像变体。Faith-linked resource; vanilla artwork varies by religion.

gfx/interface/icons/modifiers/icon_piety_christian_01.dds

宗族威望Dynasty Renown

Crusader Kings III · @dynasty_prestige_icon!

宗族层面的名望，不等于个人威望。Dynastic reputation, distinct from personal Prestige.

gfx/interface/icons/modifiers/icon_dynasty_prestige_01.dds

影响力Influence

Crusader Kings III · @influence_icon!

部分政体使用的政治资源，并非所有角色都拥有。A political currency restricted to relevant government mechanics.

gfx/interface/icons/modifiers/icon_influence_01.dds

勇武Prowess

Crusader Kings III · @skill_prowess_icon!

角色的战斗能力，是属性而不是可支付货币。A combat attribute, not a spendable currency.

gfx/interface/icons/icon_prowess.dds

国库Treasury

Crusader Kings III · @treasury_icon!

本体登记的国库标识，是否出现取决于政体与玩法。A registered treasury icon; its use depends on government and feature.

gfx/interface/icons/icon_imperial_treasury.dds

时间与冷却Time & Cooldown

Crusader Kings III · @time_icon!

显示年限与冷却条件，不代表可支付资源。Represents time and cooldowns, not spendable resources.

gfx/interface/icons/icon_time.dds

帝国恩典Imperial Grace

Crusader Kings III · @imperial_grace!

原版登记名，不可直接等同阿斯塔特战士自身的恩典储量。A base-game registry; not the same as each Astartes Warrior's personal Grace.

gfx/interface/icons/icon_imperial_grace.dds
