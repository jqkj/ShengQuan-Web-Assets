一｜三件珍宝，未必能组成一身军备

在中世纪残酷的肉搏与重骑冲锋中，单件稀有宝物或许能让一位骑士个人耀眼，但真正决定会战胜负的，是一整套配合严密、毫无破绽的精钢武装。圣泉实装的精钢军械涵盖了十四类正典装备：长剑、双手剑、匕首、战斧、战锤、刺球锤、骑枪、弓、弩、盾牌、头盔、骑士冠盔、统帅权杖与箭袋。每一件都经军械师反复淬磨，不加繁复花哨的虚饰，唯求在阵线碰撞时绝对可靠。

二｜套装不是孤立的属性奖励

精钢军械的核心奥义在于「精钢军火库」（jic_jinggang_set_modifier）套装共鸣机制。当一名角色身上同时装备「至少三种不同的精钢宝物」时，套装将被正式点亮，每件精钢装备立即启用强大的进阶属性，并完全激活其附属的词缀与升华加成；若未达成三件不同种类的配齐条件，则所有部件仅能发挥平庸的基础属性，且重复佩戴同类装备（如两把长剑）仅计为一种。

三｜不同组合，不应被套进同一规则

配齐三件套的统帅更将解锁专属军略决议——「检阅精钢军械」（jic_jinggang_inspection_decision）。领主只需花费20枚金币，军械师便会收紧握带、校直钢件，为已装备的每件精钢宝物直接恢复3点耐久度（最高至耐久上限）；更令全军振奋的是，检阅将激活长达两年的「整军备战」（jic_jinggang_ready_modifier）增益状态，带来全方位的军团战斗力飞跃，冷却周期为三年。

四｜每一套装备，终究要有人穿上

精钢军械不是供奉在玻璃柜里的展品，而是必须由血肉之躯穿上走入泥泞战壕的利器。为麾下圣泉骑士团与核心将领成建制配齐三件套精钢，并定期发起军械检阅，乃是圣泉领主横扫群雄、让帝国军势威震海内的根本底气。

Story, choices and consequences

A fine sword tells only part of a soldier's story. Refined-steel equipment is strongest when several different pieces are actually worn together. Collect the items through the Treasury, then use the character artifact screen to equip them. Merely owning a selection of swords and armour in storage does not make a complete set, no matter how much it cost to acquire them.

The refined-steel system counts distinct kinds of eligible artifacts. At least three different pieces equipped at once unlock the set's enhanced attributes and full additional effects. Three copies of one kind are still one kind. Some older swords and plate pieces keep their own baseline values and may be counted in specified categories, so look at the combination rather than the item names alone.

With a qualifying set equipped, the Inspect Refined Steel Equipment decision lets you spend gold to restore part of the current items' durability and gain a temporary military benefit. It has a reuse interval. Planning the inspection before the next campaign may matter more than clicking it whenever it appears.

Dragon Knight equipment follows a different rule. Any two distinct pieces from the specified helm, greatsword and armour can activate their full combination effects; a single piece retains its basic ability. Forging and ascension can build on both equipment families, but some refined-steel affix benefits depend on the set still being worn. Taking off a key piece may return the effect to its baseline form. Decide who will actually wear the items before committing valuable stones and locks.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

如何开始Getting started

在圣泉宝物库收集相关器物，到人物宝物界面安排实际装备，再查看套装状态；精钢成套后，从决议进入检阅。主要投入是取得装备、赐福制作与检阅所需的资源，检阅另有等待间隔。套装效果始终取决于当前装备组合。

Collect relevant artifacts at the Treasury, equip them on the character, and inspect the set. Once a qualifying Refined Steel set is equipped, use the inspection decision. Acquisition, forging and inspection have separate costs, and the effect checks the presently equipped combination.

按种类收集精钢军械Collect distinct refined-steel pieces

精钢系列包括不同武器、护具与辅助器具，分别服务于作战和统御。先按人物当前的装备位置与用途挑选，再考虑如何补足组合；抽到同一种装备多次，不会因此多算一种套装部件。

The Refined Steel family includes weapons, armour and other equipment that support fighting or rule. The effect counts distinct artifact kinds, not repeated copies of a single piece.

三种不同精钢宝物的成套条件Three different pieces for a full set

同时装备至少三种不同的精钢宝物，才会启用新版精钢器具的进阶属性与完整附加收益。旧骑士长剑计入长剑种类，旧剑与旧板甲保留原有属性。只把三件放在收藏中，或穿戴三件同类物品，都不能替代种类条件。

Equip at least three different Refined Steel artifacts simultaneously. Specified older swords count within the sword category while old sword and plate pieces retain their baseline values. Merely owning items in storage or wearing three copies of one kind does not qualify.

检阅决议与耐久恢复Inspection decision and durability recovery

达到成套条件后，可以使用“检阅精钢军械”决议，花费金币修整当前装备的精钢器具，恢复一部分耐久，并取得限时的整军收益。它有再次使用的间隔，适合安排在下一段征战之前。

With a qualifying set, the Inspect Refined Steel Equipment decision spends gold to repair some durability and grant temporary army benefits. Its reuse interval makes timing important.

龙骑士装备的两件组合Dragon Knight needs two pieces

龙焰之盔、焰之巨剑与龙焰战铠中，实际装备任意两种即可使相应部件启用完整属性；单独使用时仍有基础能力。龙骑士的组合方式与精钢不同，收集时应按各自的特殊效果说明判断。

Any two of Dragonflame Helm, Flame Greatsword and Dragonflame Armour unlock their full combination effects; a lone piece retains base abilities. This is not the three-kind Refined Steel rule.

套装、词缀与升华的关系Sets, affixes and ascension

合适的器物还可以前往赐福熔炉培养。精钢的完整词缀与升华收益随进阶条件启用，卸下关键部件后可能重新落回基础状态；决定锁词条或投入材料前，先想好人物长期会穿戴哪几件。

Refined Steel affix and ascension gains may depend on the complete set remaining equipped. Removing a key piece can revert effects to the baseline. Before locking affixes or spending materials, choose a long-term wearer and intended loadout.
