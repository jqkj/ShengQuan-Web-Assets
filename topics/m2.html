一｜一个孩子继承的，不只是父母的名字

一位泉水之裔降生时，家族往往会先寻找父母身上最耀眼的特质，盼望孩子能够原样承接。但泉水并不按照这样简单的方式传递力量。后代的结果与亲本组合有关，同一家庭中的孩子也未必拥有相同起点。某个阶段出现的强大先祖，不能被误解为所有后嗣都必定获得他的最高阶身份。

出生后的特质，是一个人故事的开端，不是它的全部。血脉浓度及阶段变化另有维护过程，人物的经历、所处规则与身份会参与其中。拥有泉水血脉，不等于血脉必然正在增长；反过来，停留在较低阶段的人，也不必就此失去未来。

二｜改变发生在人物真正活过的日子里

战场上的功绩、身份与其他经历，会让泉水浓度在相应条件下发生变化。阶段成长并非只有晋升，衰退也属于这个系统的一部分。若你只是把它当成可以永远叠加的属性奖励，就会错过这一设计最重要的意味：力量同一个人的处境联系在一起，也会随着命运的转折重新受到衡量。

管理血脉时应区分三件事：出生传播决定下一代可能从哪里开始，浓度变迁决定活着的人怎样变化，外貌显示则决定这些身份是否在肖像上显现。三者拥有不同的规则开关：当前主模组的浓度变迁默认限于玩家近亲家庭，扩大到整个宗族须另选规则；V3.1.6.1 龙角的显示范围则默认是当前玩家宗族。肖像上出现龙角，不意味着血脉浓度已自动增长，更不代表单凭年龄就能晋阶。

三｜泉水之龙：让身份拥有可见的轮廓

V3.1.6.1 的「泉水之龙」为符合条件的华夏传承角色加入身份龙角。泉水之裔的前四个阶段具有各自角型，圣女、战争侍女、骑士和战神则按身份获得成熟角型与配色。它不是能装备或出售的神器，也没有额外属性；与其说角色因此变强，不如说原本隐于特质栏中的血脉终于在面容上留下印记。

显示需要对应身份、华夏传承及「外貌与特征」中的泉水之裔外貌开关，还要符合单独设定的龙角显示范围。默认范围是当前玩家宗族；也可以选择只看当前玩家、家庭、历代登记宗族或所有合格角色。儿童并无十八岁门槛，角型依据特质而不是自然年龄变化。旧存档、冠饰穿插和部分继承场景仍需游戏实测，不宜宣传为完全兼容。

四｜真正的传承从来不是复制

当你看着第一位带有龙角的孩子长大，重要的并非他是否和祖辈一模一样，而是他将走出怎样不同的道路。血脉提供可能，个人经历决定它怎样被使用；外貌使这种关系更容易被看见，却不该取代那些发生在战争、宫廷与家庭之间的真实选择。

Story, choices and consequences

An heir inherits more than a surname, a castle and a line of claims. The Sacred Spring bloodline may also pass from parent to child—but it is not a copy of whichever parent bears the strongest blessing. The combination of biological parents and the birth-transmission rule helps determine what a child may receive. Two siblings can therefore begin with different measures of the spring's favour.

After birth, lineage is still a living history. A person's bloodline concentration responds to qualifying experiences: advancement in station and fame, prestige and piety, fortunes in war, and changes in stress can all matter. Developing an heir means deciding not only what to teach them, but what kind of life they will lead. The bloodline trait and its concentration give you two related readings of that story.

When concentration reaches the appropriate conditions, an heir may advance to a stronger stage. When the accumulated measure falls, the next assessment can instead mean decline. The blessing is therefore neither a permanent upward staircase nor a guarantee that descendants inherit the highest stage reached by their parents. Watch how the current character is developing rather than treating the family pedigree as the entire answer.

Someone born without this bloodline may still seek an initial blessing through the Sacred Spring Deeds' First Spring trials, if eligible. Sufficient valid trials across different kinds of practice lead to a deliberate acceptance, and the outcome depends on performance. At game setup, decide separately how bloodline transmission at birth works and whose concentration can change over time. The two rules serve different parts of the dynasty's story. The default concentration scope is the player's close family; the separately configured dragon-horn display scope defaults to the current player's dynasty.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

如何开始Getting started

从人物的泉水血脉特质与浓度信息查看成长；主动争取泉水之力可前往圣泉功业。血脉成长本身没有统一的购买费用，代价来自角色的经历、培养时间和承担的风险。出生传播与浓度变迁应分别设置。

Read a character’s Sacred Spring bloodline trait and concentration to follow development. Characters without the bloodline may seek the First Spring trials under Sacred Spring Deeds. Birth transmission and changes in concentration have separate game rules.

浓度怎样随经历变化How experience changes concentration

泉水之裔的浓度会随符合条件的经历变化。头衔与名望的成长、威望和虔诚的积累、战场胜负以及压力变化，都可能留下影响。培养一位继承人，也是在安排他将经历怎样的人生。

Qualifying developments in rank and fame, prestige, piety, military success or failure, and stress can change concentration. There is no universal purchase price for growth; time, actions and risk create the experience.

晋阶与退阶Advancement and decline

浓度达到相应条件后，人物有机会迈向更强的血脉阶段；积累降低也会影响下次评定，并可能带来退阶。及时留意当前浓度与人物经历，为需要重新振作的继承人安排下一步。

Reaching the proper concentration conditions can allow advancement. Falling concentration can also influence a later assessment and may cause a decline. Watch the current character rather than assuming bloodline stages are permanent.

子嗣的血脉传承Inheritance by children

子嗣的血脉结果会参考生物学父母的相关特质，并受到出生传播规则影响。婚姻与生育让泉水传承进入王朝故事，但孩子并不保证原样复制父母的最高血脉。

A child’s possible result takes relevant traits from the biological parents into account and follows the birth-transmission setting. Children do not always inherit the highest parental stage.

没有血脉，怎样争取初泉恩泽Seeking the First Spring without a bloodline

尚未承脉的人，也可以在符合条件时通过圣泉功业的初泉考验争取泉水之力。完成足够的有效考验、覆盖不同的实践方式，再亲自确认领受；不同表现会影响最终获得的血脉。

A character who lacks the trait may qualify for First Spring trials in Sacred Spring Deeds. Complete enough valid trials across different practices and explicitly choose to accept the blessing; the result depends on performance.

分别设置出生与成长规则Configure birth and life separately

开局时，血脉浓度变迁默认限于玩家近亲家庭中符合条件的泉水人物；也可以改为玩家宗族、更广范围，或关闭。出生传播是另一项规则。请注意，V3.1.6.1 龙角的默认显示范围是当前玩家宗族，与血脉浓度成长的默认范围不是一回事。

Bloodline-concentration changes default to qualifying characters in the player's close family. You may instead choose the player's dynasty, a broader eligible group, or disable these changes. Inheritance at birth is a separate rule. In V3.1.6.1, dragon-horn visibility defaults to the current player's dynasty; this is not the concentration-growth default.

泉水之龙：华夏血脉的可见印记Spring Dragon: A visible sign of the bloodline

V3.1.6.1 起，符合华夏传承与泉水血脉或传说职业要求的角色，可以显现与身份对应的龙角。泉水之裔前四阶段具有不同角型，泉水圣女、战争侍女、骑士、战神则有各自的成熟角配色。龙角仅为外观，不提供属性加成，也无需装备。

先在「外貌与特征」开启「泉水之裔外貌」，再到「圣泉传说：泉水之龙」游戏规则设定可见范围。默认当前玩家宗族，也可保留历代玩家宗族。符合条件的儿童可以长角，但不会随年龄自动升级。

高髻和冠饰可能穿模；旧档首次启用、继承与存读档仍待实测。阅读 V3.1.6.1 完整版本公告和八张实机截图。

V3.1.6.1 introduces cosmetic dragon horns for eligible Chinese-heritage characters with the matching spring bloodline or legendary role. The first four Spring Descendant stages have distinct forms; Spring Maidens, War Maidens, Knights and War Gods have different mature-horn colors.

First enable Spring Descendant Appearance under Appearance & Traits, then choose the visibility scope under Sacred Spring: Spring Dragon in Game Rules. The default is the current player's dynasty. Eligible children can display horns; age alone does not upgrade them.

Headwear may clip. Old-save, succession and reload behavior need testing. Read the full V3.1.6.1 note and view eight in-game images.
