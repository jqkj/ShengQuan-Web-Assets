一｜一个终端里，可能有不止一位医生

当你选择患者并打开阿斯塔特药剂师的服务时，最容易忽略的是实际执行者。宫廷中可能有数名药剂师，他们的造诣、医疗储备与当前行动资格各不相同。领主虽然可以承担治疗金币，却不能将多名医生的储备合在一起使用。若你指定的医生不符合条件，也不能仅凭终端列出其他合格人物，就认为本次手术一定会由那些人替他完成。

因此，操作前先确定三件事：患者患有什么问题，选定的医生是哪一位，这名医生能否同时满足造诣、储备与当前状态要求。治疗类型不同，能够处理的病伤范围也不同；一次服务通常针对匹配的一项问题，而不是把患者身上所有病征全部删除。

二｜储备回复与历练成长是两本账

医疗储备（上限100，每月+5）是实际医生用于承担服务的个人资源，会在对应机制下恢复；职业历练（0/25/50/75/100 XP）则来自合格的工作与经历。医生暂时没有储备，不等于他失去了曾经获得的资历；反过来，储备充足也不保证能跳过高难度手术所要求的造诣。让一位年轻医生从较简单的工作做起，和让最资深的医生保留力量处理危重患者，都是合理的分工。

老年病调理还有病程与间隔的区别。你需要选择患者已有的具体病征，并按严重程度准备投入。病程减少，并不等于年龄下降；一种老年病得到缓解，也不能说明其他独立病征已经被顺带治愈。

三｜火种回收与植入不是同一次互动

回收发生在修士离世之后，是单次由事件引导的判定；植入则是领主为十六至二十四岁合格廷臣主动开启的十八阶段神圣改造工程。前者在成功后把一份对应军团的真实火种宝物送入领主宝库，后者则在确认开始时正式消耗一份库存中的火种。

火种本身决定了候选者今后所属的军团与职业可能。把战死者留下的火种妥善收纳，并在适合的年轻后辈面前慎重使用，是维系阿斯塔特修士传承的关键闭环。

四｜中断、失败与取消，不能只写成“退款”

植入开始时，整场结果依据本次条件确定；接下来的十八道工序（第二心脏至黑甲膜）按顺序推进，并持续检查患者、医生和主持者能否继续。关闭界面不会取消手术，也不能通过重开反复重抽。需要时，应从终端返回已经进行中的手术。

手术推进中动态判定各阶段成功与死亡风险；若遇非致命排斥，受术者进入90天「植入排斥疗养」期。若过程中因参与者失效等原因中断，系统仅向原执行医生退回未进入后续阶段的医疗储备，火种与虔诚绝不退还。

Story, choices and consequences

At the Apothecary's station, three things that sound like a single legacy must be kept separate: the doctor's personal medical reserve, the doctor's professional experience, and the Legion gene-seed held as an actual item. The reserve measures what can be spent on care today; experience measures which difficult work the doctor can attempt; the seed identifies a Legion that may be carried forward. A competent doctor with no reserve cannot borrow a colleague's reserve for the same operation.

Medical services cover different needs. A patient might need one existing disease or wound removed, a year of health support, relief from stress, attention to an ageing condition, or a difficult life-extension operation. Each service has its own recipient rules, physician skill threshold, payment and cooldown. Most direct treatments remove one matching condition, not every illness at once. Monthly recovery replenishes reserve; only qualifying work develops experience.

When a direct courtier with one clear Legion affiliation dies, an eligible Apothecary may have a chance to recover their gene-seed. This is not limited to battlefield deaths, and the physician chosen for the event may differ from the one you were inspecting in the terminal. Success creates a real, unequippable seed item owned by the ruler. It does not give stats and does not automatically turn another courtier into Astartes. The ruler's own death is outside this particular direct-courtier recovery route.

Implantation is a further decision. Select an eligible courtier aged sixteen to twenty-four and a senior Apothecary with enough personal reserve, then provide the actual seed and required piety. The seed fixes the Legion; a profession is chosen only if the procedure succeeds. Its eighteen stages continue after the window closes, and the outcome is determined at the beginning, not rerolled by reopening the screen. Normal failure may cause injury or death and does not automatically refund the seed, piety or preparation. If the procedure is interrupted because participants become unavailable, only eligible reserve associated with stages not yet entered can be returned to the original physician, within their capacity.

医疗、回收与植入各消耗什么What do treatment, recovery and implantation consume?

药剂师把今日的伤者与军团的明日连在一起：医疗储备决定现在能拿出多少准备，历练决定能够承担多难的工作，火种则决定哪一份军团传承能够继续。把它们分别看清，才能知道一次救治、一场回收或一项植入究竟需要什么。

There are three separate resources: a particular doctor’s reserve, that doctor’s experience, and an actual Legion gene-seed item held by the ruler. Treatment requires the physician’s skill, gold and their own reserve; recovery checks a dead courtier’s seed without spending reserve or experience; implantation consumes the real seed, piety and the selected physician’s reserve.

医疗储备的归属与恢复Who owns the medical reserve and how it recovers

医疗储备归每一名药剂师自己所有，会随着时间逐步恢复，并有容量上限。刚进入职业的修士有起始储备，但储备充足并不能替代专业造诣。一个医生够熟练、另一个医生有储备，并不能拼成同一次合格手术。

普通专业互动可以指定实际执行者；确认前检查这名医生和患者是否都能行动。植入期间，医生与患者还会受到各自的占用约束。药剂师死亡或失去职业之后，其储备不会自动转给另一人，也不会变成继承人的宫廷物资。

三类病伤治疗会帮助医生积累历练；成功完成火种植入也会给予历练。月度恢复的是储备，回收火种也不会把历练当燃料扣除。

Each Apothecary has a personal reserve with a starting amount, capacity and gradual monthly replenishment. Professional experience is separate and cannot be borrowed from another doctor. The actual actor and patient must both qualify; the continuing procedure locks the relevant participants while under way. A dead or former Apothecary does not transfer their reserve to another healer or an heir. Qualifying disease/wound treatments and successful implantation develop experience, while simply recovering reserve or collecting a seed does not.

死亡后的火种回收Gene-seed recovery after death

在你的直接宫廷里，若一名拥有明确且唯一军团归属的人死亡，游戏会检查是否有合格药剂师可以接手回收。疾病、意外和其他死因同样可能触发这项检查。统治者本人死亡不走这条“直接廷臣”的回收路径。

能够承担任务的玩家药剂师会优先接手；否则从合格廷臣药剂师中选择回收把握较高的人。回收并不必然使用你此前在终端预览的那位修士。事件出现后，你可以尝试保存，也可以放弃；技艺提高成功机会，却不能保证每一次都带回火种。

成功得到的是一件属于你的实际基因种子物品，带有对应军团身份。它是传承凭证，不能装备，也不自带角色属性。留在物品栏里并不会自动产生新阿斯塔特，下一步仍需要安排一名患者与一位医生。

When a direct courtier with a single identifiable Legion dies, a qualified Apothecary may be chosen to attempt recovery. Illness and accidents can trigger it as well as battle; the player ruler’s own death is not part of this specific route. A qualified player Apothecary is preferred, otherwise an eligible courtier with better prospects may be chosen. Accept or decline the recovery opportunity. Success creates a real Legion-specific seed item, which cannot be equipped and gives no inherent character attributes.

植入的候选者与医生条件Candidate and physician eligibility for implantation

火种植入面向你直接宫廷中十六至二十四岁的合格候选者。候选者需要满足改造资格，具备最低健康条件，尚未拥有阿斯塔特职业或军团身份，并能自由接受手术。它不是为玩家本人安排的这一条植入入口。

执行者必须是资深药剂师，拥有充足的个人医疗储备并能够持续参与。你还需要拥有一枚有效的军团火种，满足主持者、冷却与其他仪式条件；已有进行中的相关升格或植入时，不能再重叠启动。

确认开始后，所选火种会被消耗，虔诚与医疗准备也随之投入。火种决定军团，候选者的健康与实际医生的造诣共同影响这次手术的机会。成功以后，才从这个军团允许的职业中选定新人今后的职责。

The candidate must be a qualifying courtier in your direct court, aged sixteen to twenty-four, with sufficient health, no current Astartes profession or Legion identity, and able to undergo surgery. This specific implantation entry is not for the player character. The surgeon must be a senior Apothecary with sufficient personal reserve and able to continue. You also need an owned, valid seed, the piety and the required actor/cooldown conditions; overlapping ascensions and implants are prohibited. The seed fixes the Legion; only on success do you select an allowed profession.

连续工序、失败与中断返还Eighteen stages, failure and interruption refunds

植入开始时，整场结果已经依据本次条件确定；接下来的十八道工序按顺序推进，并持续检查患者、医生和主持者能否继续。关闭界面不会取消手术，也不能通过重开反复重抽。需要时，应从终端返回已经进行中的手术。

成功会把候选者送入该军团的职业选择，并让医生获得历练。失败可能让患者进入健康暂时下降的恢复期，也可能导致死亡。正常失败不会自动退回已经投入的火种、虔诚或医疗准备。

若过程中因参与者失效等原因中断，系统只核算尚未进入的后续工序所对应的储备，向原执行医生返还可返还的部分。已投入的准备和已进入的工序不在其中；原医生必须仍然活着并保有药剂师身份，实际返还还受他的储备容量限制。火种与虔诚不会一起退还。

The outcome is determined at the beginning. Eighteen stages progress in order, repeatedly checking patient, doctor and presiding ruler. Closing or reopening the window does not cancel or reroll the surgery. Success allows an appropriate profession and grants doctor experience. Failure can cause temporary poor health or death; ordinary failure does not automatically refund seed, piety or preparation. If a participant becomes invalid and the procedure stops, only unused reserve attributed to later stages may return to the original doctor, subject to their survival, continued profession and reserve cap. Seed and piety do not return.
