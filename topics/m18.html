一｜宫廷里来了一个不同寻常的人

在圣泉照耀的诸疆域中，并非所有战士都遵循凡人骑士的体态。欧格林（Ogryn）是人类分支中极为罕见的巨型亚人种族。他们身形如山岳般巍峨，肌肉宛若顽石，挥舞重锤便能轻易撕裂最厚重的阵线。然而造物主的恩赐从来带着枷锁：巨人们心思质朴直白，难以理解宫廷政治中阴沉的密谋、复杂的外交辞令或深奥的经义学术。若领主强行将其置于政务桌前，得到的只能是灾难与困惑。

二｜真正的成长，要靠经历换来

欧格林体内沉睡着恐怖的「战场怒火」轨道（trait_track_ogryn_rage），唯有真正的血与火方能促成其肉身四阶蜕变：一阶「蛮力觉醒」赋予其澎湃蛮力与5%行军移速；二阶「厚皮硬化」抵消5点勇武惩罚并显著增益健康；三阶「山岳之躯」将骑士效能猛增20%；最终四阶「战争巨兽」则攀升至顶峰——移速提升15%、基础威慑+20，并带来骇人的50%额外骑士效能加成。这样的战争机器一旦冲锋，敌阵将不攻自溃。

三｜一位巨人的位置，不只是冲锋队列

要让巨人成长，领主必须遵循严格的实战法则：欧格林必须以「骑士（Knight）」身份亲自踏入战场搏杀，单纯担任军队指挥官是无法积累战场磨砺经验的！在战阵中，骑士胜利可获5点经验，生还获3点；斩杀8至14勇武的强敌获2点，斩杀15至24勇武的敌方骑士获5点，而斩杀伯爵以上统治者或勇武≥25的绝世冠军更可直接斩获10点巨额成长。唯有在真正的血海搏杀中，这尊巨兽才能彻底觉醒。

Story, choices and consequences

Not every formidable fighter is trained in the same way. When the Ogryn rule is enabled, an extremely rare branch of unusually large human characters may appear in the world. You do not simply select one from the ruler designer as a standard campaign option. If such a person joins the story, consider what their strength might offer your court and what other responsibilities their body and temperament make difficult.

Ogryns are defined by extraordinary prowess, health and endurance, while diplomacy, intrigue, learning and stewardship bear costs. Their speed may also be reduced early on. This mixture can make them valuable in combat and less suited to duties where subtlety and administration matter most. Equipment, experience and the risk of war remain real considerations.

The Battlefield Tempering track grows through actual knightly combat. Surviving battles as a participating knight—victorious or defeated—can earn experience, as can qualifying kills of powerful opponents in combat and duels. Holding a command position alone is not the same trigger as participating in battle as a knight, so the assignment you give an Ogryn changes what it can learn.

The stages move from awakening raw strength toward harder skin and more developed martial resilience, improving prowess, health, mobility or knight effectiveness in different ways. It is a life of practice rather than an upgrade you purchase all at once. Any Academy, profession or other special eligibility still follows that feature's own rules; the Ogryn trait does not automatically waive them.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

如何开始Getting started

开局启用欧格林规则，在出现相关人物后查看其特质与“战场磨砺”成长。主要投入是人物培养、装备与战场时间，实际参战伴随受伤和死亡风险。是否还能参与学院、职业或其他玩法，需要分别满足那些玩法自己的资格条件。

Enable the Ogryn rule at game setup. Rare characters may then appear; consider their traits, Battlefield Tempering progression and actual army role. Equipment, combat time, wounds and death remain part of their development.

启用规则并留意罕见出生Rare births after enabling the rule

开启欧格林规则后，世界里可能极少见地诞生这种巨型人类分支。遇见之后，可以通过人物本来的宫廷与军队安排让其承担合适职责；它不是从统治者设计器直接选择的一条常规开局路线。

An unusually large human branch may rarely be born. It is not an ordinary ruler-designer selection or a standard starting character class.

体魄优势与其他能力代价Physical strengths and other costs

欧格林拥有突出的勇武、健康与耐久，适合成为需要强健体魄的战场人物。与此同时，外交、谋略、学识与管理方面存在代价，早期行动速度也受影响。挑选职责时，应该一并考虑这些差异。

Ogryns gain exceptional prowess, health and endurance but face disadvantages in diplomacy, intrigue, learning, stewardship and sometimes initial speed. Choose their duties accordingly.

骑士参战与强敌击杀Knightly combat and powerful foes

作为骑士参与战斗，胜利或战败生还都能积累相关经验；在符合条件的战斗与单挑中斩杀强敌，也会推进成长。只担任指挥官，并不等于自动获得骑士参战这一项经验。

Participating in battle as a knight, whether surviving victory or defeat, can add experience; qualifying kills in combat or duels can also progress the track. A commander-only assignment does not count as knightly participation.

战场磨砺与阶段成长Stages of Battlefield Tempering

从蛮力觉醒到厚皮硬化，再向更强的战场形态成长，欧格林会逐步获得勇武、健康、行动或骑士效能方面的强化。这条道路依靠实际经历推进，不能用一次普通购买直接跳到最后阶段。

Their path develops from awakening raw strength toward hardened resilience and greater prowess, health, movement or knight efficiency. It advances through action, not a one-time purchase.
