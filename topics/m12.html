一｜龙泉不是从天而降的一支免费大军

传说中，遥远世界的龙帝保全了震旦的底蕴，也将来自天朝的战争技艺交托给泉水仙女。这份盟约让神州血脉与圣泉产生了另一种联系。但神明给予的并非一张可以随时召唤无限士兵的空白诏书：对玩家来说，龙泉需要从符合资格的家族志向开始，依照自己的政体、家主身份与相关条件逐步推进。

家族志向投入影响力，随着阶段发展带来不同的成长与军备权限。家族成员、教育与治理由此与战场产生联系。你不能因为看见一支玉勇军的图标，就以为它已成为当前领主实际拥有的部队；取得招募资格之后，仍须为兵员、维护和战争中的损耗付出代价。

二｜兵阵中的每个人，都有不同的位置

龙泉的兵阵并非一支名字华丽、功能却彼此重复的军队：初阶的龙泉刀盾手守住战线，长戟兵抗击冲锋；继而加入轻骑兵与轻弩手，赋予追击和远射不同的选择；再向上，斩马刀队与重甲龙泉弩手负责更艰难的突破。高阶志向解锁俑士龙泉禁卫、天庭龙卫的神弩卫和盾戟卫，最终才迎来「崇唐」。主模组现行脚本共定义十种龙泉兵种，并由五阶家族志向依次开放招募权；它们各有地形与克制取向，不会因为冠上「天朝」之名就没有弱点。

当家族逐步获得更高层次的军备，军队也会改变王朝如何经营财政。维持一支威武的禁军，比在宣传图上摆出整齐队列困难得多；战时动员、平时储备和将领配置，始终是领主必须亲自完成的工作。天朝技艺提供可能，真正的军势要在治理中被养出来。

三｜战争结束之后，土地仍在承受后果

龙泉体系还涉及京观等具有鲜明战后意味的选择。它不是可在每次胜利后随意领取的纯收益按钮；战争留下的威慑，与地方发展和人心的损失，可能同时存在。若将这项机制只写成“胜利即可获得强大加成”，便丢失了它本来要玩家面对的代价。

龙帝的馈赠可以帮助人类守护秩序，然而“守护”并不保证每个举动都没有伤害。你可以借强大军势抵挡入侵，也可能为了扩张而付出难以轻易修复的地方代价。王朝最终成为怎样的存在，不能仅凭军旗的颜色来判断。

四｜泉水之龙，让传承显现在人物身上

V3.1.6.1 为符合条件的华夏传承泉水人物加入龙角。前四阶段的泉水之裔使用不同形态，圣女、战争侍女、骑士与战神的成熟身份则有相应外观。它是一种无需穿戴的肖像装饰，并不增加战斗数值，也不要求角色先长到十八岁。角型来自当前特质，不因年龄自然自动升级。

这一视觉主题与龙泉军势分享文化意象，却不能被误认为招募天朝兵种的独立资格证明。血脉身份、肖像显示范围与家族志向的实际解锁条件分别判断；华夏传承是龙角的一项前提，却不代表所有符合文化的角色都会因此拥有圣泉血脉。将这几条线清楚分开，才能让玩家明白自己看到的力量究竟来自哪里。

五｜远方的盟约，最后仍落在凡人的生活里

当年轻家主第一次调动这支异世传承的军势时，他也必须像所有中世纪领主一样计算人力与粮食；当拥有龙角的孩子站在宫廷里，他依然会成长、遇险、继承或失去领地。神明把跨越世界的可能交到了人类手中，却没有把历史从他们手里拿走。这正是龙泉作为圣泉一部分最值得讲述的地方。

Story, choices and consequences

Longquan begins with a decision about the direction of your house, not with an instant army. The house-aspiration interface develops the connection between the Lady's spring and the Dragon Emperor's distant martial will. The relevant form of government and house-head eligibility are required. Its progress uses Influence, so budgeting only gold is not enough to understand what the next stage asks of you.

As the aspiration opens new permissions, you can recruit different Jade Warrior formations, elite retainers and Celestial Dragon Guard. Shields, polearms, crossbows and cavalry answer different battlefield problems. Unlocking a permission does not place a regiment in your army; look in the men-at-arms recruitment interface and pay the actual recruiting and maintenance costs.

The aspiration also contributes to the dynasty beyond battle, affecting governor development, education, examinations and related forms of administration. Some advanced units require Influence and may have different costs while raised or unraised. A ruler who spends everything gaining access may find they have too little left to sustain a campaign.

War can leave a second mark on the land. With the appropriate rule active, battles or sieges may present choices about captives, plunder and building a grim display of victory. Raising a jingguan or extracting wealth in the relevant siege event harms local development, while choosing to continue the campaign can avoid that additional damage. These consequences are tied to the specific event, not a universal result of every victory.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

如何开始Getting started

龙泉从家族志向进入，军队从兵士招募界面查看；京观由相关战争事件触发，并有自己的规则开关。龙泉以家主所处政体的家族志向能力为入口条件。主要代价包括影响力、各兵种的招募和维护资源，以及战争选择对人物与领地造成的后果。

Develop Longquan through the house-aspiration interface, then recruit unlocked men-at-arms through the army interface. The jingguan consequences come from applicable war events with separate rule controls. House-head and government conditions apply; development uses Influence.

以影响力经营家族志向Develop the aspiration with Influence

在能够使用相应家族志向体系的政体下，逐步投入影响力发展龙泉。它的进阶资源是影响力，不能只按金币预算理解；入口资格与当前升级条件仍需要在家族界面确认。

Invest Influence as the house advances through Longquan stages. Access and upgrade costs must be checked in the house interface; it is not solely a gold-based progression.

按权限招募天朝兵种Recruit eligible Cathayan formations

「龙泉」家族志向共五阶、十种已定义兵种：一阶可招募龙泉刀盾手、龙泉长戟兵；二阶增加龙泉轻骑兵、龙泉轻弩手；三阶增加龙泉斩马刀队、重甲龙泉弩手；四阶增加俑士龙泉禁卫、天庭龙卫神弩卫与盾戟卫；五阶解锁「崇唐」。

这些是逐步取得的招募权限，不是直接赠送的常备军。不同兵种有各自的战术定位、地形表现与费用，实际仍须在兵士界面招募和维护。

The Longquan house aspiration has five ranks unlocking ten defined units. Rank I unlocks shield-and-sword and halberd Jade Warriors; rank II adds light cavalry and light crossbowmen; rank III adds the heavy blade formation and armoured crossbowmen; rank IV adds the terracotta guard and two Celestial Dragon Guard formations; rank V unlocks Chongtang.

These are recruitment permissions, not free standing regiments. Each unit still has its own role, terrain profile, recruitment requirements and maintenance.

家族教育与总督成长House education and governor development

龙泉对家族成员的总督经验与儒家教育成长提供帮助，并涉及家族教育、考试与统治方面的收益。它既服务于远征，也能成为长期经营官职、教育和家族地位的一条发展方向。

Longquan can support governor experience, Confucian education, examination and administration-related benefits for the house. Its value lies in both command and cultivating the family’s government.

招募、闲置与动员开销Recruitment, upkeep and raised costs

不同兵种的招募与维护使用不同资源，部分高阶部队涉及影响力；闲置和动员时的支出也可能不同。组军前先看购买与维护要求，避免只准备了招募费用，却没有为之后的战争留下余地。

Unit groups use different recruitment and maintenance resources; higher formations may require Influence. Raised and unraised upkeep can differ, so budget both recruiting and sustaining a campaign.

京观与战后地方后果Jingguan and aftermath of war

相关规则开启并满足触发条件后，围城或战斗会带来相应选择。围城事件里的筑京观、索取财产会损毁当地发展度；选择继续进军，可以避开这一项额外损毁。不同事件的俘虏、威望与地方后果并不相同，不应只看选项名称就替整场战争作同一种决定。

With the rule and event conditions satisfied, a battle or siege can create specific choices. Building a jingguan or taking wealth in the applicable siege event damages local development; choosing to continue the advance avoids that additional devastation. Captives and prestige vary by the particular event.

泉水之龙：华夏血脉的可见印记Spring Dragon: A visible sign of the bloodline

V3.1.6.1 起，符合华夏传承与泉水血脉或传说职业要求的角色，可以显现与身份对应的龙角。泉水之裔前四阶段具有不同角型，泉水圣女、战争侍女、骑士、战神则有各自的成熟角配色。龙角仅为外观，不提供属性加成，也无需装备。

先在「外貌与特征」开启「泉水之裔外貌」，再到「圣泉传说：泉水之龙」游戏规则设定可见范围。默认当前玩家宗族，也可保留历代玩家宗族。符合条件的儿童可以长角，但不会随年龄自动升级。

高髻和冠饰可能穿模；旧档首次启用、继承与存读档仍待实测。阅读 V3.1.6.1 完整版本公告和八张实机截图。

V3.1.6.1 introduces cosmetic dragon horns for eligible Chinese-heritage characters with the matching spring bloodline or legendary role. The first four Spring Descendant stages have distinct forms; Spring Maidens, War Maidens, Knights and War Gods have different mature-horn colors.

First enable Spring Descendant Appearance under Appearance & Traits, then choose the visibility scope under Sacred Spring: Spring Dragon in Game Rules. The default is the current player's dynasty. Eligible children can display horns; age alone does not upgrade them.

Headwear may clip. Old-save, succession and reload behavior need testing. Read the full V3.1.6.1 note and view eight in-game images.
