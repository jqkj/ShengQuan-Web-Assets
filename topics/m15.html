一｜委托从一件具体的小事开始

在圣泉悬圃的一侧，矗立着一面承载着众生疾苦与誓言的巨型木榜——圣泉功业榜（jic_oath_board）。这里不是发放廉价赏赐的集市，而是考验封臣与门徒忠诚的试炼场。从偏远荒村肆虐的凶暴魔兽，到战乱荒年嗷嗷待哺的流离难民；从运粮补给的崎岖驿道，到深山幽谷中遗落的上古圣物，每一份张贴的委托，都是一段等待凡人去书写救赎的历史。

二｜愿意承担，才开始拥有故事

领主可亲临功业榜审阅告示，派遣麾下合资格的圣泉骑士、宫廷学士或阿斯塔特勇士接榜启程。每一道誓约都具备严密的履行时限与周期检查：领主必须合理评估受命者的个人能力（如勇武、谋略或管理），因为远行充满了未知的变数——遭遇伏击、补给断绝或是敌对势力的阻挠，稍有不慎便会导致任务折戟沉沙。

三｜报酬要等到该结算的时刻

当誓约顺利完成，丰厚的报偿将在大殿上庄严兑现：不仅有沉甸甸的黄金、威望与领民的感恩赞颂，更能为家族赢取至关重要的「圣泉声誉」并解锁专属的高阶圣物与特质。而若任务遗憾失败，领主亦须直面威望折损、受托人负伤受挫甚至领地动荡的严酷惩罚。

四｜别把“暂停”误以为保存待办

尤其需要全体领主铭记于心的是控制界面中的核心设计真相：功业榜控制中提供的「全局暂停」按钮，绝非将已有任务冷冻留待日后继续的存盘机制！根据源码逻辑，执行全局暂停会彻底关闭生成门，并「一次性直接清理并注销玩家已接取的全部未完成委托」；而当领主日后解除暂停时，系统将「重新张榜生成一批全新的委托列表」。厘清这一机制，方能避免因误解而蒙受巨大的誓约沉没损失。

Story, choices and consequences

The Sacred Spring Deeds turn a kingdom into a place you are asked to serve. At the Hanging Gardens, read the available commissions before accepting one. A request names a place, an obligation, a deadline and a promised reward. Taking it does not instantly send you travelling or pay the reward. Your ruler needs to hold the Gardens core and meet the active system's requirements; commissions have limits on how many can be undertaken at once.

Some requests draw you into local affairs. Investigate an irregular spring, reconcile rival users of water, arrange food and shelter for displaced families, settle a market quarrel, assist a scholar or revive a stalled construction project. Thorough work, short-term relief and paying personally may have different costs and outcomes. The decision is not simply to find the right map marker: you may need to remain in place or finish a chain of arrangements.

Other commissions ask for tangible delivery. Escort an envoy safely, bring grain where it is needed, recover a ritual object and carry it home, or travel a road to leave a record for future travellers. Artifacts and historic accounts require their own judgment. You might preserve old traces, strengthen a fragile object or follow a line of evidence whose meaning is not immediately clear. Learning, supplies, gold and time can open different ways forward.

A commission to hunt a notorious knight can unfold through reports, pursuit, investigation and combat. Success may require returning home to close the matter, while defeat can wound the ruler and fail the task. The deeds have a cost in time and personal risk; some rewards depend on how well the work was carried out, not just whether you reached the destination.

Completed secular commissions can pay gold and prestige, while Sacred Spring work may also award Dew and affect bloodline development. Newcomers without the bloodline can attempt the First Spring trials: four qualifying trials spanning at least two kinds of practice, followed by an explicit choice to accept. Dew can be spent on rest, travel equipment and lessons in martial, diplomatic, stewardship or learning fields. Read the available commission and its terms before promising what you cannot yet finish. Globally pausing the Deeds clears current commissions for all players—it is not a harmless freeze of their deadlines.

如何开始Getting started

从悬圃进入圣泉功业。领取新委托需要本人持有悬圃核心、功业规则开启、功能正在运行，并满足人物可行动、委托有效及在办名额等条件。主要投入是旅行时间、处理事件所需的资源与人物风险；部分任务还要返程或交付后才结算。

失去核心不会自动撤销已接承诺，任务期限仍会继续。界面的全局暂停会清空整局所有玩家的已接与未接委托；恢复后重新张榜，旧任务不会恢复。凝露、既有成长与未使用的商品库存会保留，使用前应读清暂停提示。

Enter Sacred Spring Deeds from Hanging Gardens. The actor must hold the core, meet the action and quota requirements, and use a running, enabled feature. Deadlines continue if the core is lost. The global Pause option clears accepted and unaccepted commissions for all players; it does not freeze them. Dew, completed growth and unused goods stock remain under the documented rule.

查看委托、期限与在办名额Read each commission and its deadline

先看目的地、期限、具体目标与约定酬谢，再决定承接。每年张榜有数量与地点条件，完成或辞去后不会立刻补出新单；已接委托则可以跨年继续。承接本身不会自动出发，也不会立即支付报酬。

Check the destination, expiry, objective and promised reward before accepting. Bulletin refreshes have limits on location and annual quantity; finishing a commission does not immediately produce a replacement. Accepting a task neither travels for you nor pays instantly.

调查、调停与地方事务Investigations, mediation and local concerns

调查失常的泉眼，协调两岸用水，为难民安排住所与粮柴，调停集市争执，帮助学者找到去处，或为停滞的工程落实筹备。完整安排、临时救急与个人出资有不同代价，也会在地方留下不同结果；有些工法还需要留驻完成。

Tasks may involve a troubled spring, rival water rights, food and shelter for refugees, market disputes, a scholar’s place at court or stalled building work. Full solutions, temporary relief and paying personally differ in cost and local outcome. Some tasks require staying on site.

护送、运送与实际交付Escort, transport and real delivery

使者需要本人安全抵达，粮食需要核验交付，寻回的礼器需要送回约定地点。也可以亲赴道路与渡口，留下供后来旅人使用的路簿。到达地图上的一点，往往只是接下来作出选择的开始。

An envoy must arrive safely; supplies and recovered ritual objects must be delivered to their intended destination. Surveying roads and crossings can leave guidance for later travellers. Arriving at a place may only begin the next decision.

鉴定、修复与整理泉史Appraisal, restoration and spring histories

器物鉴定需要核问线索，修复礼器要在耐用、保存旧痕与临时加固之间权衡，整理泉史则要面对残碑和并不一致的记忆。学识能打开部分处理方式，金币、时间或合适的材料也可能成为另一条路径。

Examine clues before identifying artifacts, choose between durability and preserving traces when restoring a ritual item, and reconcile fragmentary records when compiling spring history. Learning, material, gold and time can each unlock possible solutions.

追踪与讨伐极恶骑士Pursuit and trial against notorious knights

讨伐会经历报讯、追踪、查访与决斗，连续讨伐还要完成数段征程。胜利后按要求返家结案；败北可能让你受伤并使整份委托失败。额外表现与实际完成情况会影响回响，出发前要衡量人物的战斗能力。

A hunt can involve reports, tracking, investigation and combat, sometimes over several stages. Return as required to close the commission. Failure can wound the ruler and fail the entire task; performance affects its final echo and reward.

报酬、凝露与初泉考验Rewards, Dew and First Spring trials

世俗委托提供金币、名望等报酬，圣泉委托还涉及凝露与相应血脉成长；金额以承接时的约定为准。尚未承脉者完成四项有效初泉考验、覆盖至少两类实践后，可亲自确认领受泉水之力。凝露还能兑换休整、远行行装或军务、外交、管理、学识课业；查看服务后确认，才支付相应费用。

Secular commissions grant their agreed gold or prestige; Sacred Spring commissions can also grant Dew and bloodline progress. Unblessed characters may complete four valid First Spring trials covering at least two kinds of practice before explicitly claiming the blessing. Dew can buy rest, travel kit or martial, diplomatic, stewardship and learning lessons; inspect the service before paying.
