一｜第一份职责，是判断是否值得拔剑

在宫廷中拥有一名阿斯塔特战士（00_astartes_Warrior）之后，王朝便拥有了最坚固的重装骑士与军阵教官。通过「荣耀决斗」，合格的战士可以前往挑战符合条件的强敌，但决斗绝非安全摘取战功的仪式：被挑战者可能亲自应战，也可能推出强力冠军。战士能够战胜强敌、积累历练与帝皇恩典，却也可能负伤、被俘，甚至牺牲在擂台之上。

因此真正考验领主的，不是能否在菜单里找到决斗按钮，而是明知风险仍选择让谁前往。把一位长期陪伴王朝的老修士派上生死擂台，与让年轻战士在较有把握的目标面前积累经历，承受的是不同分量的决定。

二｜战争之外，经验还能成为他人的成长

「战术指导」让这名战士把自己的经验传给合格同伴，可能提升不同能力或授予限时训练结果。你不能指定每次一定增长哪项能力，也不能无视目标关系与再次指导的间隔。真正重要的，是老兵可以在没有战争的时候继续为宫廷留下价值。

当新的冲突临近，「策动战争」与「紧急征募令」又把他的专业知识带回军事准备。前者围绕合适宣称、对象与政治后果展开，后者在满足条件时紧急召集阿斯塔特老兵与精锐常备，在危急时刻支撑前线。

三｜守护一名同伴，也是一种战士的责任

战士积累的「帝皇恩典」（Imperial Grace）是他个人的圣洁财富。「赐福愈疗」消耗恩典处理相应创伤，「赐福能力」尝试提升人物的一项本领；「奖励忠诚」能把忠心与“忠嗣侍从”特质授予23岁以下的年轻侍从，为日后阿斯塔特升格打下根基；「血脉祝福」则为合格目标提供圣泉专属血脉。

恩典始终记在实际执行的这名战士身上，不能把几位修士的储量合在一起支付。当前储量本身还联系着军事与勇武优势，所以救急、育人和为下一场战争保留力量，有时会互相争夺同一份资源。

四｜一位老兵会留下怎样的宫廷

战士的历练分为五个阶位：0 XP 基础新兵、25 XP 老兵、50 XP 战斗组长、75 XP 连队长、100 XP 战团长。随着参战与专业经历累积，他的服役履历也会继续成长；你培养的因此不只是一个高勇武单位，而是一个能够在前线、教场与同伴之间承担不同责任的人。

开战时触发的战时专长落在战士本人身上，取决于他在军阵中担任指挥官还是精锐骑士。一位合格的老兵离开人世时，留下的不仅是战绩，还有受过他指导的将领、被他庇护过的侍从，以及因他而更加坚固的王朝基业。

Story, choices and consequences

When war approaches, a ruler wants a veteran who can meet a dangerous foe and still find time to teach the next generation. An Astartes Warrior can do both: a Glory Duel puts their own life at risk against a worthy opponent, while Tactical Instruction helps an eligible companion grow. A challenge is never a ceremonial guarantee. The opponent may fight personally or send a champion; defeat can mean wounds, capture or death for the Warrior you trusted.

Before a campaign, a Warrior's duties extend past personal combat. Arrange War works with qualifying claims and belligerents, while Emergency Muster can summon a special contingent for an eligible landed recipient. The summoned force is random, not a unit you order by name. Beginning a war yourself may also grant a temporary wartime specialisation to a qualifying courtier Warrior, but the effect belongs to that chosen character and depends on the role they actually perform.

Imperial Grace can serve people who will never stand on the front line. Grace Healing treats existing wounds; Grace Ability develops an Astartes companion; Reward Loyalty prepares a suitable young attendant, and Bloodline Blessing may change the future of an eligible person. Each action has a recipient, resource cost and limits. A ruler cannot combine the Grace of several Warriors into a single payment or promise a specific random result.

That reserve also supports some of the Warrior's own bonuses, so spending it for a wounded friend can weaken the strength retained for the next fight. Combat and professional service build experience, while the current Grace amount changes as it is spent and recovered. Cultivating a Warrior means deciding when their duty is to fight, when it is to teach and when it is to put someone else's future ahead of their next victory.

职业能力与操作边界Role abilities & limitations

帝皇恩典归执行战士本人，专业互动中的消耗不会从其他战士名下合并支付。

恩典既能用来提供服务，也影响当前储量加成；安排赐福时应一并考虑留存的需要。

战斗履历逐步改善勇武、移动、骑士作战和军队维持，部分阶段影响战场伤亡；深厚声望也能转化为勇武。荣耀名号记录已经抵达的里程碑。

当前恩典储量另外关联军事、外交、勇武和骑士效能，极为丰厚时还影响更多能力。使用恩典后，这部分依赖储量的优势也随之变化。

你主动开战时，合格廷臣战士有机会随机获得一种限时战时专长。效果落在获选战士本人，收益取决于他实际承担的角色；领主不会无条件获得这些效果，玩家本人是战士也不等于自动触发。

Imperial Grace belongs to the performing Warrior; it cannot be pooled with other Warriors to pay for an action.

Grace pays for services and also affects bonuses linked to the current reserve. Consider what you want to keep for later.

Service through war can improve prowess, movement, knight effectiveness and army upkeep, with later effects on casualties. Reputation milestones record achievements.

Current Grace also supports martial, diplomacy, prowess and knight-related bonuses; those reserve-linked bonuses change as Grace is spent.

When you personally declare a war, a qualifying courtier Warrior may randomly gain a temporary specialisation. It belongs to that character, not automatically to the ruler or every army.

荣耀决斗Glory Duel

从终端候选名单中指定外部对手发起单挑。获胜可能俘虏、击杀或击伤对手，并让战士积累历练与恩典；对方也可能派出冠军迎战。

  • 对手须有足够勇武且处于外交范围，排除本宫廷、宾客、你的宗族与近期交过手的人。
  • 败局可能导致执行战士死亡、被俘或损失恩典。
  • 此入口需要 FP1“北境群雄”内容包。

Challenge a qualifying strong external opponent through the terminal. Victory may capture, kill or wound the opponent and increase the Warrior’s experience and Grace; the target may send a champion instead.

  • Opponent must have adequate Prowess and be in diplomatic range; exclude the home court, guests, your dynasty and recent duel opponents.
  • Defeat may kill or capture the Warrior or cost Imperial Grace.
  • Requires the Northern Lords flavour pack (FP1).

战术指导Tactical Instruction

由有经验的战士指导合格对象，随机提高军事、管理或勇武中的一项，或给予其中一类限时训练效果。指导者也得到成长与恩典。

  • 对象须尚非“阿斯塔特战士”，并属于互动允许的自己、廷臣、封臣，或满足族长条件的宗亲。
  • 同一对象不能接连接受指导，具体能力与永久或限时结果不能预选。

An experienced Warrior guides an eligible recipient, randomly improving Martial, Stewardship or Prowess permanently or giving a temporary training effect. The instructor gains experience and Grace.

  • The target must not already be an Astartes Warrior and must fall within the permitted self/courtier/vassal or qualifying dynasty relationship.
  • A single target cannot receive repeated instruction immediately. The specific stat and permanent versus temporary result cannot be chosen.

策动战争Arrange War

以合格头衔为目标，安排自己或符合要求的封臣发动宣称战争；必要时先取得相应宣称。

  • 需要成熟的统治者与资深战士，目标须为符合条件的独立统治者。
  • 出征者、目标头衔及双方状态都须通过检查。筹谋会损失正统性并引来敌意，领土仍要通过战争取得。

Arrange a qualifying claim war for yourself or an eligible vassal against a qualifying independent ruler. The necessary claim may first need to be obtained.

  • Requires an experienced ruler and Warrior; target, attacker and title must all qualify.
  • Planning can cost legitimacy and provoke hostility; land is still gained through the actual war, not the planning interaction.

紧急征募令Emergency Muster

在合格有地对象的首都召来特殊部队，随机出现“聪明头”军团或克里格死亡军团。

  • 消耗实际执行战士的帝皇恩典，不能在这次互动中任选兵种。
  • 部队仍涉及补给，并可随继承传递。

Summon special troops in the capital of a qualifying landed recipient. The result randomly includes a Clever Heads regiment or the Death Korps of Krieg.

  • Spends the performing Warrior’s own Imperial Grace; you cannot choose the special regiment in this action.
  • The force still has supply considerations and can be passed through succession.

赐福愈疗Grace Healing

清除目标现有的轻伤、受伤与重伤。它适合处理明确的创伤状态。

  • 对象须在允许关系内且确实负伤；消耗战士的恩典，同一目标有较长的再次受福间隔。
  • 疾病、失明与缺失肢体应分别查看治疗或改造服务。

Remove a recipient’s existing Bruised, Wounded or Severely Injured trait.

  • Recipient must be eligible by relationship and actually wounded. Spends Grace and has a substantial recipient cooldown.
  • Disease, blindness and lost limbs belong to other treatment or surgery services.

赐福能力Grace Ability

随机永久提高外交、军事、管理、谋略、学识或勇武中的一项。

  • 目标已经属于五种阿斯塔特职业之一，消耗执行战士的恩典。
  • 不能指定这次提高哪一项能力。

Permanently increase one of Diplomacy, Martial, Stewardship, Intrigue, Learning or Prowess at random.

  • The recipient must already be in one of the five Astartes professions; spends the performing Warrior’s Grace.
  • You cannot specify which stat increases.

奖励忠诚Reward Loyalty

授予合格对象“忠诚”与“忠嗣侍从”。侍从在军事、勇武、骑士效能与同类认同方面获得优势，身份也能帮助日后的升格评估。

  • 对象须未满二十三岁、尚非阿斯塔特且没有相斥身份；战士须有相应历练，并投入自己的恩典。
  • 侍从更容易积累压力，仍须另行完成升格。

Grant an eligible young person Loyalty and Loyal Heir Attendant traits. The attendant gains support in Martial, Prowess, knight effectiveness and peer relations, and may benefit when later assessed for ascension.

  • Recipient must be younger than 23, not already Astartes, and free of conflicting identity; Warrior requires corresponding experience and Grace.
  • Attendants can accumulate stress more readily; formal ascension must still be completed separately.

血脉祝福Bloodline Blessing

按目标性别，从相应范围随机授予一种圣泉血脉。

  • 消耗恩典，主要面向自己；选择同宗成员时，你须同时担任宗族与家族首领。
  • 已有相关血脉者受到排除，不能点选想要的血脉。

Randomly grant one eligible Sacred Spring bloodline from a gender-specific set.

  • Spends Imperial Grace and primarily targets the actor; targeting a relative requires you to be both dynasty and house head.
  • Existing matching bloodlines exclude the candidate. You cannot select the exact bloodline.

战时专长Wartime Specialisations

专长限时作用于获选战士本人，包含骑士、征召、精锐、恐怖、维持与移动等不同侧重。

  • 只有符合检查的廷臣战士才可能获选，具体效果依其在战争中的身份发挥。
  • 这不是额外的主动服务入口，也不会无条件成为领主的全局加成。

On a war you personally declare, an eligible courtier Warrior may randomly receive one temporary combat specialisation.

  • Only courtiers passing the trigger are candidates, and the effect matters according to the character’s actual war role.
  • This is a conditional trigger rather than a selectable active power, and it does not automatically become a global ruler bonus.
