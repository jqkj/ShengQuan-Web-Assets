一｜升格的意义，不止是一副更强的躯体

在凡俗军队的刀枪与铁甲之外，圣泉流淌着超凡武力的终极答卷——阿斯塔特（Astartes）。升格仪式绝非单纯赏赐一副强壮的体魄，而是一场由内而外的生命重铸。领主通过侍从受洗交互，从年轻的苗子中甄选坚毅的学徒，植入神圣的基因器官，使其褪去肉体凡胎的软弱，蜕变为肩负超凡职责的战争半神。

二｜军团是出身，职业是他将承担的工作

每一位阿斯塔特都背负着双重身份：其一是十八大军团特质赋予的古老血统认同与本能战术偏向；其二则是他在宫廷与军阵中所执掌的专业分工。药剂师、随军牧师、智库馆长、技术军士与战团冠军五大专业各司其职，在各自独立的成长经验与造诣体系下，维系着阿斯塔特军团的完整运转。

三｜五种职业，各有不能合并的资源

五大专业掌控着绝不互通的专门资源：药剂师掌管基因种子与医疗储备，行使病程调理与器官手术；随军牧师维系信仰狂热与战团士气；智库馆长沟通亚空间灵能，执掌预言与精神风暴；技术军士负责精钢装备与攻城引擎的维护改装；战团冠军则主宰沙场斩首与神圣决斗。多元分工构成了全方位的战争矩阵。

四｜三条改造道路，不能互相替代

升格与深造是一场跨越数年的持续修行。从初受洗礼的战团侍从，到晋升为一至五阶的专精大师，战士的意志在一次次血战中淬炼坚固。领主需根据王朝战略，将合适的战士送入对应的专业道路，不可期待单一角色兼任全部超凡权能。

五｜力量能够延续，人物仍可能告别

然而，纵使阿斯塔特拥有远迈凡人的漫长寿命与强悍身躯，沙场的冷酷无常依然会带走忠诚的灵魂。受重伤的战士需要药剂师的紧急抢救，阵亡将士的基因种子则需郑重回收以培育新苗。唯有深刻理解这套残酷而严密的传承闭环，领主才能真正驾驭这支令整个旧大陆为之震颤的死亡天使军团。

Story, choices and consequences

An Astartes is not defined by a single gene-seed ritual. In the mod, becoming one changes a courtier's position in the realm: Legion affiliation gives them an origin, a profession gives them duties, and years of service turn those duties into a history. When war comes, such a figure may be a frontline fighter, an investigator, an engineer, a spiritual counsellor or a physician. These are five distinct paths within one broader Astartes system, not five unrelated collections of buffs.

Ordinary ascension begins with a qualifying young courtier. Review the candidate, costs and eligibility, then select an available Legion and profession. The system also permits other transformations, but they are not interchangeable: Primaris conversion strengthens somebody already Astartes; Apothecary gene-seed implantation begins from an actual Legion seed and a demanding continuing procedure. Neither is a shortcut that replaces the ordinary path's requirements, and neither makes Legion affiliation synonymous with a profession.

A Warrior risks themselves in duels and trains successors; a Librarian spends psychic power on secrets, coercion and battlefield support. A Techmarine repairs or transforms the body and maintains machinery. A Chaplain tends to traits, penance and blessings; an Apothecary treats the wounded, supports health and, as one part of medicine, can recover and implant gene-seed. Each uses specific performers, recipients and resources. The five professions are mutually exclusive for a character and Legion eligibility differs between them.

Professional experience builds through appropriate deeds and service, while personal reserves can be spent and later replenished. Recovery of a reserve is not the same as gaining experience. A longer lifespan is not immortality, and the risk of violence remains. Additional power armour, helmets and appearance pieces are managed through the separate armour add-on: acquiring the profession never silently installs the whole appearance catalogue.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

如何开始与侍从升格Getting started & Acolyte Ascension

开启《阿斯塔特》游戏规则。右键年轻合格侍从发起受洗与升格仪式，植入基因种子与器官，完成凡人向阿斯塔特超凡战士的蜕变。

Enable the Astartes game rule. Right-click qualifying young acolytes to perform the Ascension rite, implanting gene-seed and specialized organs to transcend mortal fragility.

十八军团特质与血脉认同18 Legion Traits & Heritage Identity

战士承接18大原初军团特质之一，获得专属的战术风格、属性加成与军团认同。军团特质决定角色的内在性格与最契合的职业方向。

Warriors inherit one of the 18 Primarch Legion traits, unlocking bespoke tactical doctrines, stat modifiers, and cultural identity shaping their optimal profession path.

五大专业分工矩阵Five Specialized Professions

明确划分五大专业：药剂师（医疗手术）、随军牧师（信仰士气）、智库馆长（灵能预知）、技术军士（军备锻造）与战团冠军（阵前斩首）。

Clear division of 5 disciplines: Apothecary (healing & surgery), Chaplain (faith & morale), Librarian (psychic lore), Techmarine (forgecraft), and Champion (duel leadership).

五阶经验晋阶体系5-Tier Mastery XP Progression

阿斯塔特通过战斗与本职事务积累经验，从1级入门逐步晋阶至5级巅峰大师，阶梯式解锁高深专精能力并提升战场统治力。

Astartes gain specialty XP through battle and duty, advancing from tier-1 initiates to tier-5 Grand Masters, progressively unlocking high-tier abilities and combat dominance.

基因种子回收与传承延续Gene-seed Harvesting & Chapter Continuity

战死不是终点。阵亡阿斯塔特的基因种子（Gene-seed）可由药剂师回收保管，用于在新一代侍从中开启新的升格循环，维系战团香火永续。

Death is not defeat. Fallen warriors' gene-seed can be harvested by Apothecaries to initiate future acolyte cycles, ensuring the chapter's eternal generational flame.

统治者的军团驾驭之道Imperial Stewardship of the Angels

阿斯塔特战士不受凡俗封建誓约的轻易摆布。赋予他们与其专业相称的军略重任，维系对圣泉的崇高誓言，方能铸就横扫诸国的坚固长城。

Astartes transcend petty feudal squabbles. Entrusting them with high command fitting their discipline while honoring holy oaths cements an unbreachable royal bastion.
