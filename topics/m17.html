一｜不是所有奇遇都需要拔剑

在争权夺利的残酷中世纪历史里，并非每一次命运的际会都需要拔剑厮杀。当领主从繁忙的政务与烽火中抽身，漫步于圣泉悬圃的水池边时，或许会遇见一只探头张望的温顺生灵——圣泉海獭。通过决议或偶遇故事将其收养回悬圃，领主便拥有了一位始终陪伴在身侧、用清澈眼睛注视着王朝兴衰的小小伴侣。

二｜每次回来，关系都有机会改变

每一只海獭都具备独一无二的名字（如小石、浪花、珍珠、银沫）、毛色（棕色、深棕色、银灰色），以及决定其互动偏好的四种天性（Nature）：好奇的天性引导领主探寻海岸与知识，在故事中赋予学习生活方式经验；温顺的天性专注于家园安宁，为劳碌君主带来深度的压力抚平；活泼的天性好动顽皮，为宫廷带来欢笑与声望；而忠诚的天性则羁绊深沉，在家族传承中孕育虔诚的福泽。

三｜小小的生命，也走过漫长岁月

在悬圃专设的萌宠界面中，领主可进行「投喂」、「玩耍」与「讲故事」三项主动抚慰。它们每年共享一次主要的羁绊成长反馈，使海獭的心情从最初的安静、放松逐步升温为亲近、兴奋乃至欢腾传说。为免繁杂日常打扰前线统帅，系统还贴心设计了「安静模式」：开启后被动事件频率降至四分之一。更令君主敬重的是，海獭拥有真实的年龄与自然寿命，那一抹暮年离去的伤感，赋予了这段神圣陪伴超越世俗功利的真实温度。

Story, choices and consequences

One day the Hanging Gardens may greet you with something smaller than an army or a holy relic: an otter at the water's edge, watching to see what you do. If you own the Gardens core, have no conflicting pet and satisfy the encounter conditions, you can adopt it or let the meeting end. Give it a name, and a new kind of history begins alongside the dynasty's wars and succession plans.

Feed, play with or tell stories to your companion. Each action can bring an immediate reaction, and sometimes another scene grows out of it. These three active interactions share a one-year interval for gaining another round of growth; repeated affection during the wait can still draw a response, but will not stack the same year's progress indefinitely.

An otter may be curious, gentle, playful or loyal. That temperament changes which stories feel natural and what additional gifts a committed response can earn: lifestyle experience, stress relief, prestige or piety. Growing closeness can also bring little presents, resources and occasional rarer favour, although none of them is guaranteed on every interaction.

Quiet mode reduces the frequency of passive stories without silencing your active companionship. Its records still show its name, age, character and the time you have shared. When a ruler dies, a qualifying player heir can continue caring for the same otter and keep memories of its past, though some bond must be rebuilt. The animal, too, has its own age and final farewell. That makes its place in the dynasty tender precisely because it is not an immortal decoration.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

如何开始Getting started

在悬圃偶遇后收养，再通过悬圃中的海獭入口互动。主要投入是时间、陪伴选择与成长间隔；主动陪伴没有统一的金币购买费用。出现后续事件时读清当前选项，继承人与其他宠物的情况也可能带来新的安置选择。

Own the Hanging Gardens core and look for the otter encounter, then use the otter interaction entry. Your investment is companionship and in-game time rather than a standard purchase fee. Pet conflicts, heir status and current conditions may affect the story.

偶遇与收养Encounter and adoption

持有悬圃核心、尚未拥有海獭并符合当前条件时，打开悬圃可能遇见它。可以选择留下这位小住客，也可以让相遇就此结束；收养后为它取名，陪伴故事从此开始。

Under the correct conditions, an otter may appear in the Gardens. Choose to adopt it or let it swim away. Adopted companions can be named and begin an individual story.

三种陪伴及共同成长间隔Three activities sharing one growth interval

喂食、玩耍与讲故事会带来不同的即时反应，并可能开启相应后续。三种主动陪伴共用一年的成长间隔；在等待期间再次逗它，仍能看到反应，但不会因此不断叠加新一轮成长奖励。

Feeding, playing and storytelling each yield reactions and potential follow-ups. They share a one-year interval for further growth; repeating them during the wait can show reactions but does not repeatedly grant a full new growth reward.

性情怎样影响回馈Temperament and its rewards

海獭可能好奇、温顺、活泼或忠诚。后续事件允许你进一步回应，性情也会影响额外回馈：有时让人放松，有时带来威望、虔诚或学习经验。相处方式因此有具体结果，而不只是重复同一个按钮。

Curious, gentle, playful and loyal otters gravitate toward different scenes. Committed choices can additionally yield lifestyle experience, stress relief, prestige or piety depending on temperament.

亲密、礼物与随机奖励Affection, gifts and chance rewards

陪伴积累亲密，长期相处会带来更深的羁绊，也可能收到金币、威望或宗族威望等礼物。偶尔还有更稀有的眷顾；这些都是可能发生的回馈，不是每次互动或每次检查必然掉落的奖励。

Bond grows through companionship. Milestones may bring resources or gifts, and rarer rewards may occur. They are chances, not guaranteed drops after every check.

安静模式与互动记录Quiet mode and the relationship record

可以选择安静模式，减少被动故事打断的频率，再按自己的节奏回来主动陪伴。它仍有姓名、年龄、性情与过往互动的记录，适合在远征与政务之间慢慢看见变化。

Quiet mode reduces the rate of passive stories while active interactions still work. The record follows the animal’s name, age, temperament and past interactions.

继承、年岁与告别Inheritance, ageing and farewell

主人去世后，符合条件的玩家继承人可以接过同一段海獭故事；原有名字与经历会留下，亲近则需要重新培养一部分。海獭也有自己的年岁与告别，不会因为被称作灵兽就永远留在庭院里。

A qualifying player heir can continue the same otter’s story, preserving its name and experience while rebuilding some closeness. The otter ages and can eventually die; its bond is not an eternal modifier.
