THE SACRED SPRING · PROFESSION-APOTHECARY

阿斯塔特药剂师Astartes Apothecary

诊治疾病与创伤、减轻压力和老年病、延长寿命；另有军团火种回收与植入服务。Treat specific illnesses and wounds, support health and ageing, and perform separate gene-seed recovery and implantation duties.

一｜医者首先面对的，是仍然活着的人

战场送回负伤骑士时，药剂师需要面对伤口；多年后，那位骑士也可能因为衰弱、失明或心脏病重新求助。日常治疗、<a class="story-term" href="#profession-apothecary:health-potion">健康药剂</a>、<a class="story-term" href="#profession-apothecary:night-watch">守夜安神</a>、<a class="story-term" href="#profession-apothecary:ageing-care">老年病调理</a>和<a class="story-term" href="#profession-apothecary:life-extension">增寿手术</a>分属不同服务，不能用一项“治疗”涵盖所有人物问题。针对病伤的服务按范围和难度处理具体特质，一次未必清除多个问题。

这些工作必须由真正合格的医生执行。领主能够替患者支付金币，却不能代替医生拥有足够造诣与个人医疗储备。几位药剂师的储备不会因为同在宫廷就合为一体；如果最可靠的医生刚刚完成困难手术，下一位病人可能需要等待或另寻合格医者。

二｜照料不是让时间倒退

健康药剂提供限时养护，守夜安神帮助缓解压力，老年病调理则针对特定病征逐步降低病程。病征足够轻时才有机会解除，其他独立疾病不会因此自动消失。增寿手术另有适用资格和投入，它也不会让战场、意外与其他死因从世界上消失。

照料一位老人时，最难的往往不是选择最高阶服务，而是分清哪项治疗真正能够帮助他。你可能为一位老臣争取了继续工作的几年，也可能只能在最后一段岁月中让他少受一些折磨。这样的选择比单纯追逐“永生”字样更接近药剂师职业的核心。

三｜军团的火种来自真实的人生终点

当拥有明确军团归属的合格直接廷臣修士去世，药剂师可能得到回收其火种的机会。回收并非只针对战死，也不意味着任何人物死亡后都可以随意取得火种。成功保存的是一件真实归属某军团的物品，而不是一枚穿上就能获得力量的装饰。

若选择让年轻候选人接受植入，还需分别满足患者资格、医生资历、火种持有、虔诚与医疗储备等条件。连续工序不能被当作可以任意取消重抽的小游戏；开始之后，失败与外部中断的返还规则各自不同，关闭界面不会抹去已经锁定的结果。它与普通升格及原铸化也是三条独立的道路。

四｜保存一枚火种，也是在保存一份记忆

药剂师能够救助尚在眼前的患者，也可能在失去一位战友之后为下一代保留希望。但火种的重要性不该盖过这份职业里更平凡的努力：每天诊治、等待储备恢复、照顾老去的人，以及在某些时候承认自己的能力仍有边界。战团的生命并不只存在于战斗胜利中，也存在于那些愿意守住同伴的人身上。

Story, choices and consequences

The Apothecary may be asked to treat a wounded veteran, calm a frightened companion and prepare a difficult procedure for a new recruit on the same day. These are different duties, not one generic healing button. Basic, moderate and difficult treatments each remove one qualifying condition. Health support, stress relief, ageing care and life extension address separate needs with their own eligibility and intervals.

Every doctor owns a personal medical reserve. It recovers over time, but it is not the same as experience, which records what the physician can safely attempt. A master without enough reserve cannot borrow the reserve of a novice to make one valid operation. Most services cost gold and the actual physician's reserve; some treatments improve experience, while health care and simple replenishment do not automatically teach the doctor anything.

The profession also has a duty to those who have already died. When an eligible direct courtier with a single identifiable Legion dies, a qualified Apothecary may be offered a chance to preserve their gene-seed. Death need not occur in battle. Success produces an actual Legion-specific seed item for the ruler; it is not an equippable buff and does not automatically create a new Astartes.

Implanting that seed is a separate, continuing procedure for a qualifying young direct courtier and a senior physician. The seed determines the Legion, while a successful procedure precedes the choice of an allowed profession. Eighteen stages progress after the initial decision. Their outcome is fixed at the start, so reopening the interface does not reroll it; normal failure may harm or kill the patient and does not automatically refund the resources. Interruption can return only eligible unused later-stage reserve to the original doctor, not the consumed seed and piety. In this profession, caring for the living and preserving a Legion's future are related responsibilities—but neither reduces the whole Astartes system to gene-seed alone.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

职业能力与操作边界Role abilities & limitations

医疗储备归每名药剂师本人，入职有起始储备，随时间恢复并有容量上限；月度恢复的是储备。

药剂师死亡或失去职业后，储备不会自动转移给另一医生或变成继承人的宫廷物资。

普通专业互动可以指定执行者；植入期间，医生和患者分别受占用约束。够熟练与有储备必须由同一名实际医生同时满足。

火种归持有人。回收不消耗医疗储备或历练；植入则投入真实火种、虔诚与实际医生的储备。

三类病伤治疗帮助积累专业历练，成功完成火种植入也给予历练。健康药剂、守夜安神、老年病调理与增寿手术本身不额外增加历练。

履历逐步改善疫病抵抗、健康惩罚缓解、压力消退、密谋抵抗与寿命；资深者还获得健康、生育与威望优势。

医疗储备是可以投入服务的个人资源，本身不额外提供一套属性加成。

Each Apothecary has an individual medical reserve with a starting amount, monthly recovery and capacity. Monthly recovery restores reserve, not experience.

A dead or former Apothecary does not automatically pass their reserve to another doctor or an heir.

Ordinary services may specify the performer. During implantation the doctor and patient have separate occupancy constraints; both mastery and reserve must belong to the same qualified physician.

Gene-seed is held as an actual item. Recovery costs neither reserve nor experience; implantation spends the seed, piety and the performing doctor’s reserve.

Three categories of wound and disease treatment build professional experience, as does successful implantation. Health Potion, Night Watch, ageing care and life extension do not inherently add that experience.

Career stages improve plague resistance, mitigation of health penalties, stress recovery, scheme resistance and longevity; senior physicians can gain health, fertility and prestige advantages.

Medical reserve is spent on services, not an extra stat bonus simply for remaining full.

毫无难度的治疗Basic Treatment

治疗范围包含轻伤、痛风、体弱多病等明确特质；完成后患者减压，实际医生获得历练，满熟练度时获得对应声望回报。

  • 需要金币与实际医生的医疗储备；一次只移除一项匹配病伤。
  • 患者通常须在世、自由、未旅行或卷入战争，处于可接触范围，且没有冲突中的治疗。

Remove one matching basic condition (including Bruised and several minor illnesses) and lower stress. The physician gains experience; once fully trained, an appropriate renown reward.

  • Costs gold and the actual physician’s medical reserve, and removes only one matching trait.
  • Patient generally must be alive, free, accessible, not travelling or at war, and without a conflicting treatment.

中等难度的治疗Moderate Treatment

治疗范围覆盖受伤、肺炎、失明等特质；一次清除一项匹配病伤并减压，医生获得历练，满熟练度时获得对应声望回报。

  • 需要相应造诣、金币与执行医生的医疗储备，并核对患者状态与关系。
  • 名单中的遗传性抑郁不等于覆盖所有精神疾病。

Remove one matching condition from the intermediate list, including Wounded, Pneumonia and Blind, and lower stress; the doctor gains experience or the full-mastery reputation reward.

  • Requires the proper mastery, gold and the performing doctor’s reserve; patient and relationship must qualify.
  • The genetic depression entry does not imply that every mental illness is treated.

高难度手术Difficult Surgery

处理重伤、严重疾病及名单中的特定身体问题，每次移除一项匹配特质并降低压力。实际医生获得历练，满熟练度时获得对应声望回报。

  • 需要足够造诣、金币与执行医生的医疗储备，患者须满足此项服务的状态与关系要求。
  • 一次服务不会将名单中所有现存病症一起清除。

Remove one matching severe illness or injury (including Severely Injured and other specified conditions), lower stress and give the physician experience or a full-mastery reward.

  • Needs senior mastery, gold, sufficient physician reserve and an eligible patient.
  • It removes one specified trait, not all the conditions a patient may carry.

健康药剂Health Potion

为合格患者施加一段健康增益，帮助他度过需要养护的时期。

  • 消耗金币与医疗储备，已有同类药剂养护时不能重复叠加。
  • 这项养护不会同时清除全部疾病，也不会额外增加医生历练。

Give an eligible recipient a one-year health modifier for support during recovery.

  • Costs gold and the physician’s reserve; the same care modifier cannot be stacked while active.
  • This does not clear every disease and does not itself grant physician experience.

守夜安神Night Watch and Calm

为压力达到条件的患者减少当前压力。

  • 消耗金币与医疗储备，同一患者有再次接受安神的间隔。
  • 不替换个性或清除应对习惯，这项服务本身不额外增加医生历练。

Reduce current stress for an eligible person whose stress meets the requirement.

  • Costs gold and medical reserve, with an interval before the same recipient can benefit again.
  • Does not change personality or remove coping habits, and gives no additional physician experience.

老年病调理Old-Age Condition Care

从患者已有的衰弱、心智、视力、心脏或骨骼类老年病中选择一种，使病程退回一段；所选病程已经很轻时，可以移除这一项老年病。

  • 病情越深，医生造诣、金币与医疗储备要求越高；患者须经过间隔后才能再次调理。
  • 它不倒转年龄，也不一次处理全部老年病；服务本身不额外增加医生历练。

Select one existing age-related condition involving frailty, memory, sight, heart or bones and reduce its progression; a sufficiently mild chosen condition can be removed.

  • More severe progression increases physician mastery, gold and reserve requirements; the same patient has a repeat interval.
  • Does not reverse biological age or clear every old-age condition; no additional professional experience.

增寿手术Life-Extension Surgery

药剂大师为合格对象施术，完成后长期改善健康、预期寿命与可生育年限。

  • 对象须高龄或健康极差，且尚未拥有这项增寿效果；目标条件与普通病伤治疗并不完全相同。
  • 消耗金币与医疗储备，不能反复叠加同一效果成为永生。战争、伤害和其他死因仍然存在。
  • 这项服务本身不额外增加药剂师历练。

A senior Apothecary treats an eligible older or very unhealthy person, granting long-lasting health, expected lifespan and fertile-year benefits.

  • Recipient must meet the high-age or very-poor-health threshold and not already have the life-extension effect.
  • Spends gold and physician reserve, cannot repeatedly stack into immortality, and does not remove violence or other fatal causes.
  • This service does not itself grant additional Apothecary experience.

死亡后的火种回收Gene-Seed Recovery After Death

拥有明确且唯一军团归属的直接廷臣死亡后，合格药剂师可能接到回收机会。你可以尝试保存，也可以放弃；成功获得一枚属于你的实际军团火种。

  • 死因不限于战斗，疾病和意外也可能触发；统治者本人死亡不走这条直接廷臣路径。
  • 合格的玩家药剂师优先，否则选取合格廷臣中回收把握较高者，不一定是终端此前预览的人。
  • 回收不扣医疗储备，也不消耗专业历练；技艺影响成功机会，失败会失去本次机会。
  • 火种不能装备、不自带属性，保存它也不会自动生成新人。

After an eligible direct courtier with one identifiable Legion dies, a qualified Apothecary may attempt to preserve that Legion’s seed. Success gives the ruler a real item.

  • Death may be due to illness or accident, not only battle. The ruler’s own death is excluded from this direct-courtier path.
  • A qualifying player Apothecary is preferred; otherwise the highest-likelihood eligible court doctor may be chosen, not necessarily the terminal preview selection.
  • Recovery spends neither medical reserve nor professional experience. Skill affects success; failure loses this opportunity.
  • The seed cannot be equipped, has no passive stats and does not automatically create a recruit.

火种植入Gene-Seed Implantation

由指定资深药剂师主持，投入你持有的军团火种、虔诚与医生的个人医疗储备。成功后，沿火种已经确定的军团选择允许职业，医生得到历练。

  • 对象是你直接宫廷中十六至二十四岁的合格候选者，须满足健康与改造资格，尚无阿斯塔特职业或军团身份，并能自由接受手术；该入口不为玩家本人植入。
  • 医生须有足够造诣和个人储备并能持续参与；主持者、冷却及仪式条件也须符合，相关升格或植入不能重叠启动。
  • 确认后火种、虔诚和医疗准备正式投入，结果在开始时锁定；候选者健康和实际医生造诣影响机会。关闭界面不会取消或重抽。
  • 失败可能进入暂时降低健康的恢复期，也可能死亡；正常失败不自动退还投入。

A selected senior Apothecary begins an eighteen-stage procedure with an eligible young direct courtier, spending a real owned Legion seed, piety and their own reserve. Success lets you choose a profession permitted by the seed’s Legion and gives the doctor experience.

  • Recipient must be an eligible direct courtier aged 16–24 with sufficient health, no existing Astartes profession or Legion identity, and able to undergo surgery. This entry is not for the ruler personally.
  • The doctor must have mastery and reserve and be available for the continuing procedure; presiding ruler, cooldown and overlap checks also apply.
  • Seed, piety and reserve are committed at confirmation; the result is fixed at the start and cannot be rerolled by closing the window.
  • Failure can impose temporary poor health or kill the recipient; ordinary failure does not automatically refund the investment.
  • If the procedure is interrupted because participants become invalid, only unused reserve for stages not yet entered may return to the original physician, within their reserve capacity; seed and piety do not return.
