一｜在一场繁忙的朝会之后

在严肃压抑的封建朝堂与生死悬于一线的军阵厮杀之外，圣泉亦保留了一抹充满尘世烟火气的欢愉之所——泉水刮刮乐（jic_guaguale）。当领主结束了一场焦头烂额的内阁廷议，步入圣泉悬圃时，不妨在这处趣味小站驻足片刻，用少许金币或手头积攒的免单符券，换取一场轻松纯粹的运气游戏。

二｜奖励会进入你正在经历的生活

游戏的规则直白而清脆：常规轮次每次投入500枚金币，若持有圣泉之约签到赠予的「免费刮刮乐十连」权益（jic_qiandao_icon_label_free_scratch），更可直接免单开局！卡面上整齐排列着三个神秘刮奖区域，当涂层在指尖下被层层刮开时，泉水、金币、宝石、圣剑与皇冠等标志依次跳出。一旦三格图案形成契合的排列，耀眼的奖励便当场迸发而出。

三｜有记录的运气，不代表有确定的承诺

刮开的赏赐丰富而即时：从数十上百倍翻涌而出的巨袋金币，到珍贵的锻造工匠石，甚至偶遇仙女附赠的奇珍异宝。然而明智的君主从不把刮刮乐当成支撑国家财政的根本支柱。它是一处让你在疲惫统治之余舒缓心神的避风港；克制适度的小赌能为宫廷增添欢声笑语，而沉迷盲动的赌徒则会将整座公国的金库付之一炬。

Story, choices and consequences

Even a well-run dynasty sometimes leaves a little room for luck. At the Hanging Gardens, Spring Scratch Cards reveal ten prizes over a complete round. You pay the displayed gold cost, or use a currently valid free round, and uncover the results one after another. It is not a menu where you pick the most valuable of ten prizes, and no round guarantees a legendary visitor.

Ordinary rewards can still be timely. Gold may finish a building; prestige or piety may support another plan, while renown, dread and government-specific resources such as influence, herds or merit may matter to other rulers. A prize's value therefore depends on the realm receiving it, not merely on how grand its name sounds.

Other results are temporary character modifiers, including some upright Emperor's Tarot effects. Look at the character's current modifiers and decide whether this is the time for study, government or war. Their durations differ, and a temporary boon should never be mistaken for a permanent change to a bloodline or education.

Rare outcomes may touch longevity, fertility, unusually large amounts of wealth or legendary characters. They are possibilities, not a queue you can force toward a chosen prize. Cumulative milestones and rewards from Sacred Spring Covenant may supply free rounds; check what you actually hold before starting. The cards are a diversion woven into the economy of the realm, not a dependable replacement for governing it.

如何开始Getting started

从圣泉悬圃进入泉水刮刮乐，相应规则需要开启。现行普通轮次消耗500金币；持有有效免费权益时，可按入口提示使用免费次数。一次启动会逐张揭晓十张奖券，先核对费用与可用权益，再决定是否开始。

Open Scratch Cards from Hanging Gardens with its rule enabled. A standard round currently costs 500 gold; a valid free-round entitlement can be used instead. One round reveals ten cards in sequence. Check the current price or free-use status before starting.

开始一轮十张刮刮乐A round reveals ten cards

泉水刮刮乐以一轮十张展开，按当前流程逐张给出当次奖项。一整轮不等于自选一份大奖，也没有保证必定遇见传奇来客的承诺。一次揭示可能带来惊喜，也可能不如所愿；它是给命运留的一点空隙，不是稳定收入的保证，更不能替你免去经营。

The ten results appear in sequence; this is not a choose-one-of-ten prize. A legendary person or top prize is not guaranteed in every round.

资源类奖励Resource rewards

常见收获包括金币、威望、虔诚、宗族威望或威慑等资源。适用的政体还可能遇到影响力、牧群或贤能奖励；同样一轮刮刮乐，对不同统治者可能有不同的实用价值。

Possible gains include gold, prestige, piety, renown and dread. Some governments can additionally receive influence, herds or merit-related rewards. Their usefulness depends on the ruler.

限时加成与塔罗Temporary modifiers and Tarot

有些结果会带来限时能力加成，或正位帝皇塔罗牌的影响。得到后可以查看人物身上的修正，再决定这一段时间适合学习、治理还是投入军务。奖励持续时间各异，不会全部永久留存。

Some results grant limited-time stat effects or upright Emperor’s Tarot modifiers. Check the character modifier list and its duration; these are not all permanent improvements.

稀有奖项的随机性Rare results remain random

较少见的奖项包含长寿、生育方面的恩赐，大额财富，以及传奇人物。它们是奖池中的可能结果；一轮没有遇见，也不意味着下一轮便能指定获得。

Longevity or fertility boons, large amounts of wealth and legendary visitors are possible rarities. The failure to receive one now does not grant the power to choose the next.

累计记录与免费权益Milestones and free rounds

累计记录达到条件，或从圣泉之约取得相应权益时，可以获得免费轮次。再次开始前查看入口显示的费用与可用权益，已经使用的免费机会会被消耗。

Cumulative achievements or the Sacred Spring Covenant can award free attempts. Check your current eligibility and charges before starting; spent free opportunities are consumed.
