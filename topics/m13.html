一｜泉水有了可以回去的地方

王朝的疆界在地图上不断变化，人物的名字也一代代从宫廷名册中消失。唯有少数事物能在这些变迁中留下连续的痕迹。圣泉悬圃就是这样一处地方：起初，你所获得的只是一枚与泉水相连的核心；后来，它才随着经营成为王朝日常生活中的隐秘据点。

这里并非一座购买后自动运转的万能宫殿。进入悬圃，首先取决于你是否实际持有核心，而不是有没有把它装备在某个宝物栏位。认主、持有和建设是三个不同的环节；寻找入口之前，应先辨明核心如今究竟掌握在谁手里。

二｜三座建筑，三种长期选择

悬圃的正式建筑围绕仙女之泉、圣泉宝物库与圣泉学院展开。前者使人物征召与相应的奉献联系起来，宝物库让偶然得到的器物逐渐拥有可经营的归处，学院则把一位领主对继承人和年轻廷臣的期待延伸到多年以后。它们不是三个互不相干的按钮，而是圣泉力量参与宫廷生活的不同方式。

建设需要投入，并有自己的营造过程。对眼前急需人才的领主而言，优先改善与征召有关的设施可能更有意义；对已经拥有一批年轻继承人的家族，学院的长期价值又可能更突出。你不必为了让每项设施立刻升到最高而打断王朝原本的节奏。

三｜据点的真正价值在于延续

当一位熟悉悬圃的领主离世，核心的归属与相关工程记录便成为继承故事的一部分。已有的建设维护逻辑会尝试沿玩家继承人承接，但这并不等于所有失去核心、角色切换或旧存档的情况都能自动恢复。若某项入口突然消失，先检查持有关系和游戏规则，比反复点击装备栏更有效。

悬圃还有不少值得描绘的景物与愿景，但景物出现在画面中，不代表它已经获得独立的互动系统。现阶段能够稳妥介绍的，是有明确入口、建设与使用链路的设施。真正的浪漫，不在于宣称这座庭园包揽一切，而在于它让一场偶然的神迹，逐渐变成可以留给后来人的日常。

Story, choices and consequences

Before the Hanging Gardens become a building programme, they are a place that must recognise a keeper. Enter through the game's main interface and follow the encounter that grants the Gardens core. Access depends on who actually owns that core, not on placing it into an equipment slot. Inheritance and gifts can therefore change who can use this haven, even while its halls and pools remain where they were.

Three formal buildings give the Gardens their working purpose. The Fairy Spring welcomes recruited characters; the Treasury draws artifacts and leads toward blessing work; the Academy raises promising students. They develop separately, so the next investment can follow whatever your court needs most. Their construction takes resources and time, while the features inside retain their own prices and requirements.

Early development of the Fairy Spring presents a choice between a gold-based route and a treasury-based route. The later building path continues from that selection, and your form of government may make one more suitable than the other. Meanwhile, recruitment contributes construction discounts that can ease future work on the three buildings without reducing every cost to zero.

As buildings advance, recruitment, forging and education can all benefit. A level-five Treasury with the necessary core ownership rules excludes two dangerous ascension outcomes; a more developed Academy can reduce special-admission waiting times. The Gardens also connect to Covenant, Scratch Cards, Deeds and the occasional otter encounter. Some older named facilities still appear in records, but their former character interactions are not presently open—architecture and artwork should not be mistaken for an available button.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

如何开始Getting started

从游戏主界面的悬圃入口打开，按认主故事取得核心，再查看可建设与可进入的区域。主要投入是建设资源和营造时间；各玩法另有自己的规则与费用。核心属于谁、相关功能是否开启，会决定当前能做哪些事。

Open Hanging Gardens through the main game interface, follow the ownership encounter and obtain the core. Check which buildings and facilities can be constructed or accessed. Resource costs and construction time are separate from the rules and prices of each connected feature.

取得并持有悬圃核心Obtain and retain the Gardens core

从主界面的悬圃入口开始相遇与认主故事，取得悬圃核心后经营这处据点。访问权看的是核心实际归谁持有，不要求先把它装备在某个槽位；转赠或继承时，也要留意核心的去向。

Access follows actual ownership of the core, not whether it is equipped. When the core changes hands by gift or succession, access may change as well.

安排三座正式建筑Develop the three formal buildings

仙女之泉支持人物征召，圣泉宝物库衔接抽取与赐福制作，圣泉学院为培养下一代提供基础。三座建筑分别发展，可以先补足当前最需要的能力，再把资源投入下一处建设。

The Fairy Spring supports recruitment, the Treasury supports artifacts and forging, and the Academy educates courtiers. Their building paths are separate; invest according to your realm’s needs.

仙女之泉的建设路线Fairy Spring construction path

仙女之泉从一级升向二级时，要在金币与国库路线之间作出选择，之后沿既定方向继续营造。选择前先想好当前政体与长期经营方式；它会影响后续建设安排。

The step from the first to the second level involves a choice between gold and government treasury routes, and later construction continues accordingly. Review your government and long-term resource plan first.

建筑升级改变哪些服务What upgrades improve

建筑成长会改善各自服务：召唤、赐福与教育都能从相应建设中获益。宝物库达到五级并满足核心持有条件时，升华会排除神弃与崩毁两种结果；学院成长也会缩短特招等待。角色召唤积累的营造折扣，则可减轻三座正式建筑的后续费用。

Building levels improve recruitment, forging and schooling. With the core and a level-five Treasury, two dangerous ascension outcomes are removed; a stronger Academy shortens special-admission waits. Recruitment discounts also reduce later construction subject to minimum costs.

据点入口与设施开放状态Which facilities are currently active

你可以在悬圃找到征召、宝物、刮刮乐、圣泉之约与功业等相关入口，也可能在开窗时遇见一位意外的小住客。月露泉池、垂藤密廊、花冠宴亭与观星高台保留设施记录；它们原有的角色交互目前不开放。

The Gardens lead to recruitment, artifacts, Scratch Cards, Covenant, Deeds and an occasional otter. Moon-Dew Pool, Hanging-Vine Gallery, Flower-Crown Pavilion and Star-Gazing Terrace remain in legacy records, but their old character interactions are not currently available.
