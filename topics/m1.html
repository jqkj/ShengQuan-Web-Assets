一｜并非每位访客都因命运而来

年轻的领主常会遇到内阁与军阵的匮乏：国库缺人打理，边防缺少善战之士，宫廷尽是平庸之辈。向泉水仙女献上奉献，正是为了在这些缺口面前寻求另一种答案。圣泉征召并不要求你盲目追逐稀世之才，而是邀请你先回答一个现实的问题：现在的王朝究竟最需要谁？贤才池寻访谋略与内政之臣，勇士池寻觅沙场骑士，战争侍女池呼唤荡清尘埃与洗净人心的武运化身，圣泉祭司池则引向神圣信仰的护道者。四个征召池拥有独立的累计计数，回应着领地不同维度的渴望。

二｜奉献只是开始，留下谁才是选择

奉献资源有着严密的支付逻辑：常规征召消耗金币，当政体与法令契合时可优先扣除国库（Treasury）或牧群（Herd）；勇士与侍女额外消耗威望，祭司则额外消耗虔诚；特殊资源不足时，系统将依规则折算金币补足。单次召唤便于细致考量，五连与十连则展开批量名册。至关重要的是：候选者显现于水镜之上，并不等于他们已经加入宫廷。领主必须在关闭窗口前逐一确认招募；未被接纳者将随水雾散去流落四方，无法事后追溯。

在四池的法则中，祭司池蕴含着特殊的机运：四池分别记录累计奉献次数，而唯有祭司池在单抽达到特定保证条件时，下一次单抽必定显现传奇的「泉水圣女」（quanshuishengnv）；五连与十连不享有此单抽保底。此外，每次征召都会唤醒「圣泉的骄矜」，持续折减悬圃建筑升级所需的金币费用并加速工期；装备神器「仙泉无尽之杖」更能使批量抽取触发持续的增益累计。

三｜仙女的馈赠不是免于经营的特权

从泉水中走出的是有命运与野心的凡人，而不是冷冰冰的数字奖励。一位杰出的追随者来到宫廷后，仍需经历职务任命、联姻、领地分配甚至生老病死的考验。仙女愿意回应虔诚的祈请，但怎样让这些门徒发挥所长、怎样防范他们的离心，终究是凡人君主必须亲自作答的治理功课。

Story, choices and consequences

Your council needs someone who can steady a troubled province, and the frontier needs a knight before the next campaign. Sacred Spring Recruitment offers another way to find them instead of waiting for wanderers to appear at court. At the Fairy Spring, decide whether you need scholars and advisers, warriors, Battle Maidens or priests. Each pool answers a different need; the most unusual visitor is not automatically the most useful person for your realm.

After an offering, the spring presents candidates, not instant courtiers. In a single summon you examine one person; in groups of five or ten you choose which arrivals to keep. Their traits, abilities and identity matter more than the spectacle of the draw. Anyone you leave behind departs, so make your choices before you close the result screen. Costs also depend on the pool and your government: prestige may support warriors and Battle Maidens, piety supports priests, and gold or other permitted resources cover the relevant offering.

Recruitment reaches beyond the people you welcome. Summons gradually earn construction discounts for the Hanging Gardens' three formal buildings, and legendary arrivals contribute more. Finding the right person today can therefore help build the Fairy Spring, Treasury or Academy tomorrow. Discounts never erase the buildings' minimum costs, however, and not every treasury resource substitutes for every kind of offering.

The priest pool has a particular promise, with conditions that are easy to miss: its qualifying single-summon count can lead to a guaranteed Saintess on the next single summon. Five- and ten-summon batches do not provide that same guarantee. The pools track their counts separately, and the result of one pool cannot simply be used to select a rare figure from another. The best use of the spring is to plan whom you need, understand the price and then decide whom to bring home.

如何开始Getting started

从圣泉悬圃进入仙女之泉，再选择角色征召。相关规则需要开启，人物也须满足当前按钮的行动条件。主要代价是奉献资源；连抽后还需要亲自决定招募哪些来客。

Open Hanging Gardens → Fairy Spring → Recruitment. Enable the appropriate rule and check the character’s eligibility. Prepare the required offering, then decide whom to recruit before leaving a batch result.

选择四种征召池Choose one of four recruitment pools

贤才、勇士、战争侍女与祭司分属四个召唤池。想补足内阁、寻找战场上的强手，或结识圣泉的传奇人物，可以先按需要选择奉献方向，再查看来客的实际能力、特质和身份。

Scholars and advisers, warriors, Battle Maidens and priests each come from a separate pool. Compare candidates’ skills, traits and identity with your court’s immediate need before committing resources.

单抽、五连与十连Single summons, groups of five and groups of ten

单次召唤适合逐人查看，五连与十连会展开一整组结果。你可以只招募其中几位，也可以全部留下。离开结果窗口前完成选择；未招募者会离开，不会自动成为廷臣。

A single summon reveals one candidate. Groups of five or ten display several, and you may retain only some. Those not retained leave; appearing on the result screen does not automatically make somebody your courtier.

奉献与支付资源Offerings and payment

常规费用涉及金币，符合政体与设置条件时也可使用国库或牧群。勇士和战争侍女另涉及威望，祭司另涉及虔诚。特殊资源不足时可能转用金币，奉献前应查看当前费用和支付方式。

Gold is a common cost. When government and settings permit, treasury resources or herds may be available. Warrior and Battle Maiden pools also use prestige; the priest pool uses piety. Where the special resource is unavailable, gold substitution may apply. Check the actual confirmation price.

征召带来的营造折扣Construction discounts earned through recruitment

角色召唤会积累悬圃营造折扣，传奇来客带来的积累更多；仙女之泉、宝物库和学院的建设费用会受其影响，但各有最低价格。召唤所得的人才与之后建起的家园，因而可以互相支撑。

Summoning characters builds a discount for the Fairy Spring, Treasury and Academy. Legendary arrivals contribute more, but each building retains a minimum construction price.

祭司池的保证规则The priest pool’s guaranteed result

四个征召池分别记录累计次数。按本页所用资料，祭司池的单抽达到保证条件后，下一次单抽会出现圣女；五连与十连不提供同样的圣女保证。想依靠这项保证取得圣女，应使用祭司单抽，而不要把连抽次数理解为同一保证。其他稀有结果仍受各自概率与条件影响，不能指定下一位来客。

Each pool maintains its own summon count. When the qualifying priest single-summon threshold is met, the next single summon guarantees a Saintess under the documented rules. Five- and ten-summon batches do not grant the same Saintess guarantee. The other rare outcomes remain probabilistic.
