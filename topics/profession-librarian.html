一｜一个人说出的，未必是他知道的全部

宫廷里最危险的事情有时并不是公开的叛乱，而是一份始终无人说破的秘密。智库可以尝试<a class="story-term" href="#profession-librarian:mind-probe">心灵探查</a>，在合格目标确实持有尚未被你知晓的秘密时揭开一角。探查不会凭空制造新的证据，更不应被写成无条件窥尽天下所有人物内心的能力。你必须先决定究竟为何需要知道这件事。

若希望直接改变他人的行事空间，<a class="story-term" href="#profession-librarian:will-domination">意志支配</a>、<a class="story-term" href="#profession-librarian:psychic-domination">灵能支配</a>和<a class="story-term" href="#profession-librarian:soul-domination">灵魂支配</a>提供不同强度与持续方式的牵制。它们存在造诣、目标位阶、成功与冷却限制；所谓支配也通过游戏内的牵制与相关机制发生作用，并不是把整个人物永久变成不受任何规则约束的傀儡。

二｜施术之后，代价不会自动消失

失败的支配并非一场不花代价的彩排。实际智库的灵能可能照样耗去，目标关系也未必按你期待的方向变化。施术者已有的能力越高，你能够尝试的道路或许越多，但“能够尝试”与“已经成功”仍隔着一次必须承担结果的行动。

当智库走向战场，<a class="story-term" href="#profession-librarian:battle-inspiration">战斗鼓舞</a>能帮助合格的修士暂时发挥更强能力，<a class="story-term" href="#profession-librarian:psychic-curse">灵能诅咒</a>却可能在失败时反噬自己。<a class="story-term" href="#profession-librarian:psychic-strike">灵能打击</a>更加危险：面对已经负伤的目标，后果可能远超过对健康人物造成初次创伤。玩家应当在下令之前看清目标当前状态，不能把所有灵能攻击都理解为一项轻微、可反复试探的削弱效果。

三｜塔罗并没有把未来交给你挑选

智库还能主持<a class="story-term" href="#profession-librarian:emperor-tarot">帝皇塔罗</a>牌。普通占卜同时涉及正位与逆位，<a class="story-term" href="#profession-librarian:sacred-emperor-tarot">神圣占卜</a>提高了门槛并将结果限定为正位，但没有因此提供自由选牌的权力。有些正位也可能伴随代价；占卜是否适合这名目标，仍需要结合现实中的战争、病痛与人物安排作判断。

无论探查、支配、鼓舞还是塔罗，占用的都是实际智库自己的灵能。资源恢复需要时间，专业历练与现存储量也不是同一件事。不同出身的军团可能提供协同，却不能使几名智库的力量任意合并成一口共同水井。

四｜真正的洞见不是控制一切

一位老智库会见过无数领主试图通过预言避免错误，也会看到有人因一个秘密而毁掉自己最珍视的关系。他能够给予情报、牵制或暂时的力量，却不能替你承担决定之后的一切后果。智库之所以值得培养，不是因为他让世界变得完全可控，而是他帮助你在越来越复杂的世界里看见更多真实的选择。

Story, choices and consequences

A perfect-sounding answer may still conceal the one fact a ruler needs. Ask a Librarian to probe a mind when you need a hidden secret; consider one of the three forms of domination when you want leverage over an unwilling character. Mind Probe reveals information; Will, Psychic and Soul Domination create different kinds of hooks, each with its own scope, difficulty and duration. None is an unrestricted switch for taking possession of someone else's character.

Psychic power can also enter the battlefield. Battle Inspiration supports another Astartes; Psychic Curse may weaken an enemy but can rebound on its caster. Psychic Strike is especially consequential. Against an uninjured person it can inflict a wound of varying severity; against someone already carrying one of the matching wound traits, the result can be death. Before using it, make sure you are prepared for that outcome rather than thinking of every strike as minor harassment.

Divination offers a different way to spend the same power. Ordinary Emperor's Tarot may turn upright or reversed; Sacred divination by a sufficiently advanced Librarian draws only upright cards and lasts longer. The reader chooses the recipient, not the result. Even an upright card may impose an unwelcome health cost, and a longer duration can make a poor fit harder to ignore.

All these services use the performing Librarian's own psychic reserve. A failed domination or curse may still consume it, and waiting for its recovery cannot undo a death, curse or Tarot outcome that already occurred. Experience and reserve are separate; Thousand Sons affiliation has its own recovery synergy. The real question is where limited psychic power best serves your court today: information, leverage, protection or a decisive change in someone's fate.

English long-form copy has not yet received the same final source-alignment pass as the Chinese edition.

灵能属于实际施术的智库，支配失败和诅咒失败仍会消耗资源。

资源以后可以回复，已经发生的死亡、诅咒与牌面效果不会因此撤销。

专业履历逐步改善学习、生活方式成长、虔诚、声望和部分宫廷关系，也帮助抵抗密谋与消退压力。更高虔诚还能成为学识的来源。

心灵探查Mind Probe

尝试揭示目标持有、而你尚未掌握的一项秘密。

  • 消耗执行智库的灵能，目标须确实拥有秘密。
  • 如果这些秘密你已全部知晓，探查不会创造新的情报。

Attempt to reveal one secret held by the target that the ruler has not yet discovered.

  • Uses the performing Librarian’s psychic reserve; the target must actually hold a secret.
  • If all relevant secrets are already known, probing creates no new information.

意志支配Will Domination

施术成功后，让你获得对目标的弱牵制。

  • 目标最高头衔不能高于公爵，施术者须满足技艺与当前灵能要求。
  • 存在失败与再次施用间隔；失败仍消耗灵能。

On success, gain a weak hook on the target.

  • Target’s highest title cannot exceed duke rank; caster needs qualifying mastery and reserve.
  • Can fail or be on cooldown; failure still spends psychic power.

灵能支配Psychic Domination

施术成功后，让你获得对目标的强牵制。

  • 需要更成熟的智库和更多灵能，目标须低于国王位阶。
  • 有冷却并仍可能失败，失败仍消耗灵能。

On success, gain a strong hook on the target.

  • Needs higher mastery and greater psychic reserve, and the target must rank below king.
  • Has cooldown and may fail, still spending psychic power.

灵魂支配Soul Domination

由最资深的智库尝试授予长期保留的牵制。

  • 目标同样须低于国王位阶，消耗大量灵能，失败也不免除消耗。
  • 作用通过游戏的牵制系统实现；能做哪些事，仍取决于哪些行动允许使用牵制。

An expert Librarian attempts to create a long-lasting hook on an eligible target.

  • Target must rank below king. Costs much psychic power even on failure.
  • The hook works through CK3’s hook system, not arbitrary total control; it only helps actions which allow that hook.

战斗鼓舞Battle Inspiration

为合格阿斯塔特提供限时勇武、战场优势、骑士效能、军队伤害与攻城增益，并帮助消退压力。执行智库也能获得历练。

  • 对象必须是另一名合格阿斯塔特；消耗灵能，同一目标有再次受益间隔。

Give another eligible Astartes temporary benefits to Prowess, battlefield advantage, knight effectiveness, army damage and siege capability, while easing stress. The Librarian also gains experience.

  • The recipient must be a different qualifying Astartes; costs psychic power and has a recipient reuse interval.

灵能诅咒Psychic Curse

成功时随机向目标施加一种诅咒；失败时，智库本人承受随机诅咒，而且反噬持续得更久。

  • 目标不能是皇帝位阶，灵能无论成败都会消耗。
  • 诅咒结果随机，必须同时考虑反噬对现有施术者的影响。

On success, apply one random curse to the target. On failure the Librarian suffers a random curse with a longer backlash duration.

  • Target cannot be emperor rank; psychic power is spent regardless of outcome.
  • Curse selection is random, so consider what backlash could do to your own caster.

灵能打击Psychic Strike

对没有伤势的目标随机造成不同程度的创伤；目标如果已经带有轻伤、受伤或重伤，这次打击会直接导致死亡。成功执行也让智库获得历练。

  • 消耗执行智库的灵能。下令前应检查目标已有伤势，不能把它一概当成轻度削弱。

Against an unwounded target, inflict a random severity of injury. Against someone already carrying Bruised, Wounded or Severely Injured, this strike causes death. The Librarian gains experience from execution.

  • Spends the performing Librarian’s reserve. Inspect existing wounds before ordering this power; it is not necessarily a minor weakening effect.

帝皇塔罗牌占卜Emperor’s Tarot Divination

随机抽取正位或逆位牌面，为选定人物施加限时影响，并给实际施术者相应回报。

  • 消耗智库灵能，限制短期重复占卜；不能直接点选结果。
  • 正位死神会损害健康，逆位恶魔则在其他不利效果之外帮助压力消退。审判当前没有逆位结果。

Randomly draw an upright or reversed card for an eligible recipient and grant its temporary modifier, while rewarding the caster.

  • Consumes the Librarian’s power, has a reuse interval and cannot choose a card.
  • An upright Death card can harm health; reversed Devil, despite other costs, helps stress reduction. Judgment currently has no reversed result.

神圣帝皇塔罗牌占卜Sacred Emperor’s Tarot Divination

最资深的智库可以使用只含正位结果的占卜，影响持续更久，实际施术者获得相应回报。

  • 仍消耗灵能并限制短期重复施用，结果依然随机。
  • 它使用上方牌表的正位列；正位死神的健康代价同样存在。

A senior Librarian draws randomly from the upright card pool for a longer lasting effect; the caster receives a corresponding reward.

  • Still spends psychic power and has a reuse interval; outcome is random.
  • Uses the upright column of the card table, including the health cost of upright Death.
